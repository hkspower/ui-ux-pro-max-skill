# AHMED — the story

Guidance for anyone — person or assistant — working on this game. **This is
the canon**, and it applies to both builds: the browser game in this folder
and the Unreal port in `../ahmed-fighter-ue5`.

Recorded 2026-09-07, from the game's author.

---

## The story

**A boy called Ahmed, who loves MMA.**

He grows up on it and he is good at it. Young — younger than anyone expects —
he turns professional. That is the whole first act: a kid from the alleys who
fights, and wins, and is somebody.

**Then he falls.**

He is walking. Nothing is happening. There is a hole in the street, and he
goes down it.

**He comes out somewhere else.** The outer world.

And in that place the only thing he has is the one thing he brought: he can
fight. So he does. He fights his way through it, and as he goes he finds
**talents**, he gains **levels**, and he buys **upgrades** — which is not a
game system bolted onto a story, it is the story. He arrived with nothing but
his hands and he is building himself back up, one fight at a time.

---

## What that means for the game

The three progression systems already in both builds are not mechanics that
happen to be there — they are the plot:

| System | What it is in the story |
| --- | --- |
| **Talents** — found, never bought | What the outer world teaches him. Each one is a thing he could not do before, and finding it opens ground he has already walked past. |
| **Levels** — earned by fighting | Him getting his edge back. He was a pro; the fall took that, and every fight gives a piece of it back. |
| **Upgrades** — bought with XP | Him choosing what kind of fighter he becomes here. He does not have to rebuild the same fighter he was. |

The Metroidvania shape follows from it too. He is not touring nine places in
order — he is **lost in one connected world**, walking back over ground he
could not pass the first time because he has since learned something. That is
what the sealed routes mean.

---

## What is not decided yet

Written down honestly rather than invented, because the answers change what
gets built:

- **Where the nine areas are.** They are currently real Kuwait — Souq
  Mubarakiya, Salmiya Gym, Sharq Fish Market, Kuwait Towers, Marina Crescent,
  Failaka Island, Jahra Road, Desert Camp, Kuwait Arena — and the per-stage
  story text is grounded in that (a crew leaning on traders, the club wanting
  to see what the souq kid can do). Are these the outer world wearing Kuwait's
  face? Or is Kuwait a prologue and the outer world a second set of areas?
- **What the outer world is.** Whether it is fantasy, a warped Kuwait, an
  underground, a dream, or somewhere the game never explains.
- **Whether he is trying to get home**, or whether that stops mattering.
- **Who AL-SAQR and AL-WAHSH are** in this version. They are currently a
  street enforcer and a title-fight champion, which reads differently if the
  Arena is not in Kuwait.
- **Where the fall happens.** Before the Souq, or somewhere in the middle.

None of this has been changed in either build. The nine areas, their story
text and the README all still describe the earlier framing — a young Kuwaiti
fighter working his way to the national title. **Reconciling them is a
separate job that has not been done and should not be done without being
asked.**

## Names

- **AHMED / أحمد** — the fighter. Spelled *Ahmed* in the game's text and
  code; the author also writes it *Ahmad*.
- **AL-SAQR / الصقر** — the Falcon. First boss.
- **AL-WAHSH / الوحش** — the Beast. Final boss.
