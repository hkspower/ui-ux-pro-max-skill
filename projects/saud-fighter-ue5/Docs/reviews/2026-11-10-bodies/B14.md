## Scenario
Bare shoulders (the Boss in a singlet, Zayos stripped to the waist), rest pose and guard, from the front, the back and above: the arm leaves the shoulder as a narrow stalk and flares into the biceps.

## Evidence (finder)
Radius from the humerus to the first surface, up-and-out (the deltoid's side), cm at 1.80 m scale. At the cap (t 0.10-0.20 of the upper arm) against the arm at t 0.40: Saud 6.8 vs 6.2 (cap 1.10 of the arm), Thug 5.9 vs 5.0 (1.18), Saqr 5.6 vs 5.8, Boss 5.6 vs 6.9 (0.81), Zayos 5.1 vs 7.3 (0.70; in his own centimetres 7.5 vs 10.8). To the front: Zayos 6.4 at t 0.20 -> 10.9 at t 0.40 (9.4 -> 16.1 cm real within 9.9 cm of arm, a 34 deg flare), Boss 7.0 -> 10.2, Saud 6.6 -> 7.2. Zayos's deltoid is smaller than Saud's at equal height (5.1 against 6.8) under an arm 0.531 m round at 1.80 scale (Saud 0.421). Cause read in the code: the field's weight is 0 up to 12 % of the arm and full at 22 %, 'so the shoulder stays the size it is', while the limb is x1.21 (Boss) and x1.43 (Zayos) below it; the browser, the source of truth, thickens the limb from its root (bone(..., 9.6*bd, 7.4*bd, 5.8*bd)). The clay views show both men's arms attached by a thin neck with no rounded cap, Saud's cap standing over his arm.

## Finder's fix sketch
Let the arm's thickening begin at the shoulder joint (ramp (0.0, 0.90) over about 0.08 instead of (0.12, 0.90) over 0.10) so the cap grows with the arm it caps; or, leaving the field alone, grow the 'delt' mass with the man's limb factor (pipeline.MASS boss about 1.40, zayos about 1.65, where both are 1.15). Either is a surface change: the shoulder joint lies on the arm's axis and the clavicle joint is 14.8 cm from it, outside the rule's 11.5 cm reach, so no joint moves. Rebuild the Boss and Zayos (and re-check the armpit and the singlet's armhole on the Boss).

## Finder's test sketch
Hold on every built man at his own size: the deltoid's lateral radius at t 0.10-0.20 at least 0.9 of the arm's at t 0.40 (Saud 1.10, Thug 1.18; today Boss 0.81, Zayos 0.70). Sabotage: the ramp put back to (0.12, 0.10) with MASS delt 1.15 -- Zayos must fail.
