### Their reasoning
I tried to refute it and could not for the thug or ZAYOS; AL-WAHSH I do not count.

(a) Reproduced. My own sections give the finder's numbers within 0.5 mm on all six men, and the cause is as stated. In anatomy.build_field (lines 1647-1668) the trunk rule moves a point by its own x times (t - 1) from zc 1.00 up, while under zc 0.96 the whole pelvis moves by the hip joint's 0.092 x (t - 1). The outer hip at x 0.19 therefore moves half as far as the waistband 4 cm above it.

(b) Seen.
- Thug: in my clay views (front, three-quarter, back) the band is sunk in like a girdle and the trousers jump out under it to a square shelf on both sides, front and behind.
