### Their reasoning
Real on ZAYOS only; the finding overreaches on AL-WAHSH and on the shoulder line.

**Reproduced.** The core numbers come out the same to the millimetre with a different sectioning method: ZAYOS's chest is 0.758 x 0.360 m (2.11, and 2.26 at zc 1.24) and his waist 1.81, against 1.43-1.76 and 1.18-1.59 on the other five. The code agrees: in `anatomy.build_field` the torso block writes only `P[:, 0]`, so trunk depth takes the stature factor h and nothing else, while the limbs thicken radially by l. My overlay of the six sections divided by h shows one shared front and back line with ZAYOS's outline far wider.

**Visible.** At full-figure fight scale (about 640 px for the man):
- Rest pose from the side: his upper arm covers almost the whole trunk, with the chest barely ahead of the biceps and the back barely behind the triceps.
- His guard from either side: the bare trunk above the waistband is a slim column on legs that are deeper than it; the upper arm is as thick as the torso beside it.
- Three-quarter: the chest reads as a wide plate with a thin flank.
- Beside the mock with the depth term, the difference shows at that scale without zooming.

This is the wrong silhouette for the man the record calls the giant, "half again the size, which is his whole identity". He is the final heavy boss, seen from every side because the camera is a boom the player swings.

### Their fix advice
**Where:** `Tools/blender/hero/anatomy.py`, the torso block of `build_field`'s F and `scale_to`'s mesh loop. No joint has to move.

**The term:** scale y about y = 0 (the pelvis, spine and neck joints lie there) by d = 1 + 0.7(t - 1), masked by the same (1 - arm_w) so the arms ride unchanged, with the width's upper fade (zc 1.49-1.52). That gives ZAYOS d = 1.217: chest about 0.44 m deep (1.73:1), waist 0.38 (1.49:1).

**Keep it out of the joint path.** F is not only used on meshes:
- `scale_to` runs the joint table and the hand joints through F. The clavicle joint (canonical y -12 mm, partly outside the arm mask) would move 2-3 mm.
- `posture.generator` runs the joints and ears through `build_field`'s F and holds the export's joints to it within 0.5 mm ("rebuild him" otherwise).
- `scale_to` also takes the crotch pin through F, and `build_fighters.py` (line 476) takes the thug's trousers through it.

So make the depth an opt-in argument (for example `F(P, depth=False)`, with only `scale_to`'s mesh loop passing True). The 62 bones and every clip then stay identical; assert the largest joint difference is 0.

**Do not reuse the width's lower band.** The width fades in over zc 0.96-1.00 because the legs are carried out by the hip's displacement below it; depth has no such partner. With that band my mock put the waistband at 0.467 m deep over thighs of 0.383, a belt shelf front and back, deeper than the chest. Fade the depth in over the waist instead (zero at zc 1.00, full at about 1.12). The hips then stay as built (0.387 at zc 1.00) and the profile reads chest 0.44, waist 0.38, hips 0.39-0.40.

**Must not disturb:**
- The browser's width rule, the leg carry and `MIDLINE_KEEP`.
- Saud, the brawler and AL-SAQR (t = 1, so the term is exactly 1 and they need no rebuild).
- The canonical-base checks (`--physique-check`, `--neck-check`, `--back-check`) run before the field and are unaffected.

**Scope:** the need is ZAYOS's. The same formula gives AL-WAHSH 1.108 (barely visible in my A/B) and the thug 0.90 (under a tee). Take them along for consistency, or gate the term to t > 1 if three rebuilds are too many.

**To check on the real rebuild** (my mock is not a build):
- Body-shot contacts and the pair clips, against a chest about 4 cm further forward on ZAYOS and 1.7 cm on AL-WAHSH. The guards stayed clear in the mock (forearms 76 mm and 48 mm off the trunk).
- The trousers' waistband against the deeper belly.
- The deltoid-to-pec blend where the arm mask fades.

This term does not change the level shoulder top; that comes from the width stretch and is a separate matter.
