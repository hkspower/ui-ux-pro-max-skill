## Finder's fix sketch
garments.soles(): loft the outsole from the upper's own plan instead of a cube -- anatomy.shoe's half-widths plus 3-4 mm (heel about 7.8, ball about 10.4, a round toe of 4-5 cm radius), 2 cm thick, a little toe spring. anatomy.shoe(): end the toe near y -0.225 so the shoe is about 30 cm, keep 5.5-6 cm of toe-box height to within 2 cm of the tip and round it there, raise the rows at y -0.02..-0.08 to an instep of 10.5-11.5 cm falling to the toe box, add a heel counter to 9-10 cm, and give the collar a padded ring about 9 cm across round the ankle instead of the ankle's own 6.5. The ankle and ball joints stay. One dependency: build_saud.SOLE_MODEL / SOLE_HALF_WIDTH (the clips' grounding corners) and SaudPlants.h were calibrated on this slab -- keep the sole's bottom at z 0, its 2 cm thickness and the heel and ball contact lines, and re-measure the corner model if the front edge moves.
