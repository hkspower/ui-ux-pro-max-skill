## Finder's fix sketch
No existing joint needs to move, but skin weights alone cannot cure a twist under linear blending: this one needs a joint ADDED or the roll shared out. (a) Add the mannequin's own forearm roll joint (lowerarm_twist_01_l/_r, child of lowerarm at about half its length, no translation), weight the distal half of the forearm skin to it by distance in rig_export.bind_all (0 at mid-forearm, 1 at the wrist, the hand keeping only the last centimetre), and have rig_full_ik.strip_for_export / motion_ik.to_actions bake it each frame with half of the hand's twist about the forearm axis; the 62 joints and every existing channel stay as they are, the clips are re-baked for two new bones. (b) Whether or not (a) is taken, cap the clips: in motion_ik Author.arm hold |hand twist| under about 60 degrees by giving the excess to the lowerarm's own roll (invisible on a straight punching arm) and the upper arm's, and take out the 143-166 degree spikes at the Jab's frames 3/7 and the Cross's 4/9, which are the roll going the long way round between the guard's palm-in and the contact's palm-down.
