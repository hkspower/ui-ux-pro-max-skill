Each man in his own guard (elbows bent 109-134 deg, which is every idle and block frame), seen from the side of the arm; the same on any strike that bends the arm.

Through the bend, point to crease: Saud 8.1 cm at rest, 4.8 and 3.9 in the guard; Zayos 16.6 -> 7.9. The cause on the mesh: along the back of Saud's elbow the forearm bone's share of the weight is 0.04 at 5 cm above the joint, 0.28 at 1 cm above, 0.45 at the joint, 0.82 at 2 cm below, 0.94 at 5 cm below -- an even 10 cm blend with the point of the elbow shared half and half (Zayos the same shape). Pictures: all twelve elbows in the guard are a thin rounded fold with no point (a pinched dimple on Saud's). Trial, in memory only, nothing saved: the back of the arm from 3.5-6.5 cm x h above the joint downward given to lowerarm and faded round to the sides -- Saud x1.01 and 1.11, Zayos x0.77 and 0.85, a pointed elbow and no tear in clay (the corner is still boxy: the heights want tuning).

## Finder's fix sketch
Skin weights only, no joint and no new bone. In rig_export.bind_all(), after legacy.bind and beside the toe-box and crotch repairs: the olecranon is the forearm's. For each arm, over the vertices within about 9 cm x h of the elbow along the upper arm's axis, on the BACK of the arm (fading to nothing at the sides; the crease left to heat) raise lowerarm's share from 0 at 6.5 cm x h above the joint to 1 at 3.5 cm above and below, taking it from upperarm; the garments follow through the existing weight transfer. Tune the two heights on clay in the guard and on a straight arm (a punch at contact, 152-171 deg) so the cap neither boxes nor dents.

## Finder's test sketch
In rig_full_ik.verify (or the build's render stage): pose the man's guard, cast from the elbow joint along the outward bisector of the two bones and hold the point of the elbow at 0.85 or more of its rest distance (now 0.38-0.59), with the same distance within 5 % at rest and no arm face stretched over 2.5x. Sabotage: skip the repair (heat alone) -- 0.43 on Saud's right elbow, 0.47 on Zayos.

### Their reasoning
The measurement is correct and I reproduced it on all six, but it fails the visibility test, so I am not confirming it.

- **Reproduced:** the point of the elbow sits at 0.38-0.59 of its rest distance in every guard, from an even linear blend across the joint. A real olecranon keeps its distance, so the reference is fair.
- **Not recorded:** CLAUDE.md mentions elbows only for stance, skin paint and "wrinkles over the olecranon"; nothing decides or parks elbow skinning.
- **What the clay shows up close:** in clipped profile views at 8 px/cm (closer than any game camera at 1080p), all twelve elbows are clean, smooth, rounded corners. They read as soft and pointless, not as collapsed, torn or pinched. The exception is a 2 cm pinch dimple on Saud's left.
- **The title overstates it:** "thinnest exactly where it bends" is the chord from the point to the crease, an interior measure where two tubes cross. In silhouette the arm does not neck at the bend; what is lost is the outer corner, 1.6-2.3 cm on Saud and 4.3 cm on Zayos.
- **At fight distance:** I rendered whole figures at 2.5 px/cm (more generous than the game's 1.7 px/cm at 1080p) from the left, right and back three-quarter, as built and with a trial that keeps the point. I could not tell the two apart without side-by-side crops at 2x.
- **In the project's own title render:** Docs/renders/menu-title-3d-4x3.png shows Saud's lead elbow in near profile at about 3 px/cm and it reads as an ordinary bent arm.
- **The fix is not a free win:** my trial hand-over turned the Boss's 134 degree elbow into a folded beak, uglier than what is built.

This is real linear-blend volume loss and a legitimate close-up polish item. It is not a fault a player would see at the distances the game shows a fighter.

### Their fix advice
Only if the owner wants it as close-up polish; I do not recommend it as a fault fix.

- **Where:** Tools/blender/hero/rig_export.py, bind_all(), after legacy.bind and beside the toe-box repair. Weights only: no joint moves and no new bone.
- **What:** on the back of each arm, within about 9 cm x h of the elbow, shift part of upperarm's share to lowerarm. Fade it to nothing by the sides and leave the crease exactly as heat gives it.
- **How far:** aim for about 0.75-0.85 of the rest distance, not 1.0. A full hand-over is what made the beak and fold lines on the Boss's left elbow at 134 degrees, and a boxy corner on Saud and Zayos.
- **Tune on:** every man's guard, including the Boss's left at 134 degrees (the worst case), and a straight punch at contact (152-171 degrees) so the cap does not dent.
- **Must not disturb:**
  - the 62 joints;
  - the upperarm + lowerarm weight sum per vertex, which the iron-arm mask reads (re-run its check);
  - hand and clavicle weights;
  - the garments' weight transfer (the elbows are bare skin on all six, so sleeves should not change; confirm with --cloth-check);
  - the head, crotch and toe-box repairs.
- **Re-run:** rig_full_ik.py --bite and the stance cases.
