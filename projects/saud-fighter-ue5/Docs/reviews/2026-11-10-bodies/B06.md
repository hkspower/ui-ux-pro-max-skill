### Their reasoning
Reproduced on the built mesh, visible, and not recorded as decided or left.

What holds:
- The glove bends with the finger bones. 65 % of its weight is on finger and thumb bones, and the closed fist that every clip keys moves its surface up to 20 cm, folds 59 % of its faces by more than 30 degrees, caves the palm side in 5 cm and shrinks its girth from 66.5 to 51.7 cm. My clay views show a faceted wedge from the back and a hook from the thumb side, with the fingers as a beak over a hollow.
- The glove does not cover the fist it is unioned over. glove()'s docstring says the fingers vanish inside it; they are its front surface, 4-5.5 cm proud of the glove's own face, and my palm-side view at rest shows four finger stubs. The project's own zayos-apose-3d.png shows the same.
- In his guard the gloves are smaller than the forearms under them (0.79-0.91 as seen, 51.7 cm round against 62) and sit on a 28-29 cm neck above a 44 cm cuff ring. At 700 px full figure they read as bare-sized lumpy fists on pencil wrists, not boxing gloves. He is the final boss and only ever punches, so the gloves are where the eye is.

CLAUDE.md records boxing gloves as the intent ("The pipeline builds bosses") and the 2026-09-23 thumb fix; nothing records the fingers showing, the crumple or the size as accepted.

Corrections to the finder's evidence:

### Their fix advice
No joint moves. The finger and thumb bones come from the joint table hand() returns (rig_export FINGERS reads jl), not from the finger mesh. The parts depend on each other, so do them together.

1. Tools/blender/hero/rig_export.py, bind_all(): after legacy.bind / own_the_head / pin_crotch, beside the toe-box move. For a gloved man only, take every glove-slot vertex's index_/middle_/ring_/pinky_/thumb_ weight onto hand_l / hand_r, leaving heat's hand-to-lowerarm blend at the wrist. It needs the glove slot or a gloves flag passed in. It must be gated: the other five men's fingers must keep heat's weights so their fists still close.

2. Tools/blender/hero/anatomy.py, glove(), and its call at hero/assembly.py:1027. Once rigid, the glove is seen for ever in its rest shape, so the envelope must enclose the built fist.
   - Palm-side face: at least 0.054 x scale in front of the axis from t 0.6 to the tip. The sf shift now goes toward the back of the hand and leaves the face at +0.026 at t 0.84.
   - Tip: past the middle finger's joint, at least 0.135 from the wrist canonical; it now ends at 0.130.
   - Wrist and cuff rows: as wide as the forearm's end on him.
   - Scale: pass a scale from assembly.build for his build. Hold the glove at about 1.0-1.1 of his forearm's greatest width, not 1.3.
   - Thumb: the rest thumb points 9 cm behind the hand, and a rigid sleeve would freeze there. Build the thumb pad where FIST_THUMB folds the thumb (across the fingers) and leave hand()'s thumb tubes out of the union for a gloved man. The 2026-09-23 decision still binds: no bare thumb may stand out of the glove, at fist 0 or 1.

3. Tools/blender/hero/pipeline.py (the 7.5 cm capsule, lines 675-697): size the glove material's reach to the new glove and stop it at the cuff's rim, not 0.42 L plus the capsule's cap up the forearm.
