## Scenario
The striking knee of the Knee clip's pose (knee angle 69 deg here; CLAUDE.md records 64-65 in the clips), seen from his side and three-quarter front. Any knee bent past about 90 deg (falls, get-up, the run's swing leg) takes the same loss; the guards (136-160 deg) do not.

## Evidence (finder)
Ray from the knee joint along the bisector of thigh and shin to the outer surface, rest -> knee strike: kneecap side Saud 6.4 -> 3.6 cm (56 %), Thug 5.2 -> 2.6 (50 %), Brawler 6.8 -> 3.8 (56 %), Saqr 7.4 -> 4.0 (54 %), Boss 9.9 -> 5.6 (57 %), Zayos 14.3 -> 8.3 (58 %); back of the knee 59-65 % (Saud 6.9 -> 4.0). That is cos(half the bend) = 0.57, linear-blend collapse (the armature modifier has preserve-volume off, as Unreal skins). In the guards the kneecap keeps 90-100 %. Cause measured on the weights: the calf's share across the right knee goes 10 % -> 90 % over 9.0-11.4 cm canonical on Saud, Thug, Brawler, Saqr, 12.4-13.0 on the Boss, 14.6 on Zayos, 50 % within 1 cm of the joint, and the same on the kneecap side as behind (Saud front 9.9 / back 10.5 cm). Pictures: the bend is a round tube elbow narrower than the shin under it, no kneecap corner, the thigh's top line dips into it; on Zayos a ragged crease across the top of the bend where the trousers' knee folds are crushed. A real bent knee is the squarest, most prominent point of the leg, and it is the surface this strike lands with.

## Finder's fix sketch
Weights only. In bind_all after the heat bind, a pass 'own_the_knee' (lengths x h): on the front half about the hinge axis give the kneecap block -- from about 3 cm above the joint to 5 cm below, within the knee's width -- to the calf whole, and narrow the thigh-to-calf blend there to about 3 cm sitting just above it; leave heat's wide blend on the back half so the hollow behind the knee folds softly. The kneecap then turns rigidly about the joint and keeps its 6.4 cm. Check the Knee pose and a 90 deg bend in clay; the trousers follow through the transfer.

## Finder's test sketch
