### Their reasoning
The fault is real on ZAYOS; on AL-WAHSH I could not confirm it as something a player would see.

**Reproduced.** My own sectioning, which separates the trunk by geometry rather than by the finder's arm-weight cut, gives the same numbers on ZAYOS: a 44-degree corner at zc 1.13-1.14, a flank that then runs out at 39 degrees, and a lat edge that is a flange about 45 mm thick with a notch 19-31 mm deep in front of it. So it is not an artefact of where the arm was cut.

**Seen in my own clay renders** (Cycles, 8 samples, orthographic, three suns):

- Trunk, front: a ragged-edged slab behind the rib plate on each side.
- Trunk, back: two shield-shaped slabs with a level ledge at armpit height, each ending in a pointed lobe over the waist.
- Flank, front-quarter: a thin blade standing off the ribs with a deep dark slot in front of it.
- Flank, back-quarter: a faceted, pointed lump hanging over the waist.
- In his guard, from his left front-quarter and from behind: the blade and the lump are still there.

The project's own textured renders show it too: `Docs/renders/zayos-apose-3d.png` has the ragged lobes on both flanks, and `zayos-guard-3d.png` a jagged dark edge under his left arm. He is 2.66 m tall, bare-chested and the last boss, so this is on screen at fight distance.

### Their fix advice
**Where.** The fault is data: the canonical lat in `Tools/blender/hero/anatomy.py`, `BACK_MUSCLES["lat"]` (lines 134-135). It has five links, its widest (65 mm across, x `BACK_WIDEN` 1.25) low at z 1.228, and it ends at 1.118 still 40 mm across. `assembly._masses` (lines 161-166) seats it on `trunk_surface` and builds it with `chain`. `anatomy.build_field` then multiplies every sideways dimension of the trunk by the man's torso factor (ZAYOS 1.31), and he has no cloth over it.

**Smallest sound fix.** Reshape those links the way `PHYSIQUES["lean"]` was reshaped on 2026-10-03:
- Seven links, the widest high under the arm (about 1.30).
- The across shrinking evenly, the last link at about 1.095 with nothing proud, so the lower edge runs into the waist.
- The side edge wrapping onto the flank instead of standing off it.

Judge it at ZAYOS's factors on bare skin, not only on the canonical body. The canonical step is about 7.9 mm per cm and passes the 8 mm rule at scale 1, yet becomes 10.3 on him. To hold 8 on ZAYOS the canonical step must be about 6 or less, and the section at zc 1.16-1.24 should lie no more than about 8-10 mm inside its own hull on the scaled man (it is 19-31 now).

**Must not disturb.**
- The skeleton and the joint table: no joint moves, and `build_field` stays as it is, since every man and every joint goes through it.
- `TRUNK_ROWS`: the `PROPORTIONS` bands (waist, chest, hips) and the tee's drape hang off them.
- `PHYSIQUES["lean"]`: Saud has his own lat and is already right, so he needs no rebuild.
- The erector entry.
- `check_back`'s three rules: furrow at least 2.5 mm; the lat at least 6 mm proud of the loft at (0.120, 1.22) and (0.150, 1.29); no bulge over 2 mm going down from 1.30 to 1.14.
- AL-SAQR's `BODY_LOOKS` `v_min` of 1.29: a thin margin, plain 1.276, with his lat mass at 0.95.
- The `MASS` note that ZAYOS's lat at 1.10 broke the V.
- The tee and singlet drape that hangs from the lat (`garments.fit` slope 0.35, `ARM_CLEAR`, about 15.5 mm off the waist).

**After the change.** Re-run `--back-check`, `--physique-check` (its "lats' fin" sabotage builds an old lean lat), `--cloth-check` and `--grim-check`. The canonical rows changing means the thug, the brawler, AL-SAQR, AL-WAHSH and ZAYOS are all rebuilt whole. `--resume` is not an option: the lat is built before the checkpoint, and its rows are not in the `.man` stamp, so a stale checkpoint would not be refused.

**If only ZAYOS should move.** The alternative is a per-man lat, but that is more plumbing, not less: `PHYSIQUES` entries are treated as lean (`assembly.py` line 1065 asserts `check_physique` for any physique, and `bands()` reads its `BANDS`), so a second physique would need those paths split first.
