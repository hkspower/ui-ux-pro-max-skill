## Finder's fix sketch
Surface only: the field scales radially about the hip-knee-ankle line, so the joints (which lie on it) do not move and the clips stand. In build_field give the leg a profile along its length instead of the flat weight: keep the full factor on the thigh's belly (upper and middle third) and the calf's belly, and dip it over the knee -- w *= 1 - 0.5 * exp(-((u - u_knee) / 0.06) ** 2) -- so a knee takes about half the excess (Boss x1.11, Zayos x1.21, Thug x0.90). Bring the thigh in sooner (ramp 0.40 -> about 0.25) and, so the trouser tops do not step out from under the pelvis again (the reason recorded for 0.40), carry the pelvis block between canon 0.90 and 1.00 in depth by the same share. Preview the three men's fields on the Brawler's mesh (seconds, as m11.py does) before any build.
