## Scenario
Rest pose and guard seen from the side or three-quarters, as the swinging camera shows a boss: the thighs and calves stand in front of and behind a trunk no deeper than Saud's.

## Evidence (finder)

Chest breadth x depth: Saud 39.5 x 24.8 (1.59), Thug 35.1 x 24.5 (1.43), Brawler 41.2 x 26.5 (1.56), Saqr 43.6 x 28.1 (1.55), Boss 55.8 x 31.8 (1.76), Zayos 75.8 x 36.0 (2.11). At 1.80 m scale every man's chest is 24.5-26.5 cm deep whatever his build (Zayos 24.4), though Zayos's is 1.30 times as wide. Thigh depth over chest depth: Saud 0.80, Thug 0.75, Brawler 0.82, Saqr 0.84, Boss 0.93, Zayos 1.12 (thigh 40.4 cm deep against a 36.0 cm chest); calf depth over chest depth: Saud 0.62, Boss 0.75, Zayos 0.96 (34.7 cm). Zayos's waist is 56.3 x 31.2 cm. A muscular man's chest is about 1.45-1.6 times as wide as deep and always deeper than his thigh. The clay profiles show Zayos's thighs standing forward of his belly and his calves as far back as his seat, the trunk a flat slab between; the Boss the same, milder; in the side view of the guard Zayos's lower body reads twice the bulk of his upper.

## Finder's fix sketch
Give the torso rule a depth term through the same band as its width: y scaled about the trunk's own mid-depth by 1 + 0.5 (t - 1) (Zayos x1.155: chest about 41.6 cm deep, 1.82 wide to deep; Boss x1.08), faded out by arm_w, below the hips and at the neck exactly as the x term is, applied to the body, the top and the trousers together. Apply it to the meshes only and leave the joint table out of it: the spine's joints are on y = 0 and would not move, the clavicle and shoulder joints (y -12 and -6 mm) would move 1-2 mm, and the 62 joints are to stay where they are. Rebuild Zayos and the Boss; check_cloth and check_legs_apart at his size afterwards.

## Finder's test sketch
Hold on every built man at his own size: chest breadth over depth at 1.30 h at most 1.9, and mid-thigh depth no more than chest depth (Saud 1.59 and 0.80; today Zayos 2.11 and 1.12, Boss 1.76 and 0.93). Sabotage: the depth term switched off -- Zayos must fail both.
