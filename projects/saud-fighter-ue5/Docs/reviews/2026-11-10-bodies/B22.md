## Finder's fix sketch
Skin weights only, no joint moved. In own_the_head keep the skull rigid (face, jaw, ears, cranium: head_held stays 1.0) but slope the rigid zone's lower edge instead of cutting it level at 0.60 of a neck-length under the head joint: 0.60 in front under the jaw, about 0.25 behind at the occiput, so the nape belongs to neck_01. Spread the neck_01 -> head blend up the whole column (from the neck joint to that edge, 7 cm or more on Saud, not 3), let neck_01 reach 0.85 or more at mid-column, hand the chest over to neck_01 down the trapezius slope to about 4 cm under the neck joint, and weight the SCM and trapezius chains by the same height rule as the column under them so they are not left behind as fins. Optionally let the clips' counter-turn put more on neck_01 than the head (the 40/60 split in rig_full_ik._aim and motion_ik.fk_body), since the head's pivot is at the eyes.
