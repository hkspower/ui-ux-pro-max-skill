### Their fix advice
All of it is surface work; no joint moves (the lofts are built on Jp() and the field leaves on-axis points where they are).

1. Ridges: Tools/blender/hero/anatomy.py arm(), the branch after `if PHYSIQUE == "lean"`. Pass the two ring lists through smooth_rows(...) as the lean branch does (and segs=48). smooth_rows keeps the given rings exactly, so each man's biceps girth and pipeline.check_body_looks' LIMB_SHOW rule stand.

2. Front bias: same branch. Cut the forward shift of the upper-arm rings (sf +0.006 / +0.014 / +0.009) to roughly half, so that (ry + sf) / (ry - sf) is at most 1.25 (sf at most 0.007 on ry 0.065). rx and ry stay, so girth does not change. Extend arm_round to the five as a reported number first, and hold it only once a rebuilt man has given a clean value.

3. Shoulder cap on Boss and Zayos (check Brawler under his sleeve): grow the canonical 'delt' mass with the arm it caps. In assembly.masses() / _masses(), size it by the man's whole arm factor (LIMBS x the field's l, passed in from pipeline alongside arm_scale) as well as by MASS, growing it round and along the arm. Do not grow it up off the acromion: the deltoid was cut back on purpose in 2026-09 (the "puffed sleeve", 2.76 times too proud). Prefer this to moving build_field's arm ramp (0.12, ramp 0.10) toward the shoulder: the field scales everything within 11.5 cm of the arm's axis, so an earlier ramp also pushes the armpit, the pec's edge and the top of the lat, and puts check_back, check_neck and the tank and tee armholes at risk.

4. Leave arm()'s ring weights (1.0 / 0.05 / 0) and every LIMBS and MASS number alone unless the owner says otherwise; they are recorded decisions. If the step above the wrist is to be softened, lengthen the field's fade for the arm only. The 0.07 in build_field's `_smooth((u - u1) / 0.07)` is shared with the legs, so make it a per-limb entry. The wrist's and hand's own size must not change (Zayos's glove, the wraps, the hand rules).
