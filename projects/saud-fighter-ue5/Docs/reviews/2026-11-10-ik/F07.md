## Finder's fix sketch
Hold the old side only for a man who was already past the limit last frame in the same look. Keep on FTurn whether the look was past the limit last frame and its raw yaw (turned with the actor by Turned); when the look is taken up afresh -- another man (raw yaw jumps), the look back on after a reel, or a man who was within the limit last frame -- take Side from the sign of the raw yaw before clamping. HoldSide itself and the straight-behind sway case stay as they are.

### Their reasoning
I tried to refute it and could not: the fault reproduces with my own probe on inputs the game produces.

**Mechanism, confirmed in the code**
- `FTurn::Side` is rewritten every frame by `HoldSide` (SaudIK.h:1625-1630) from the sign of the raw yaw to whoever is looked at.
- The player's tick calls `FaceTowards(NearestOpponent)` every frame (SaudCharacter.cpp:326-331), and `FaceTowards` has a 0.99995 dead band (FighterBase.cpp:655). So the raw yaw to the presser is a residual under about 0.6-1.5 degrees whose sign is whichever way he last drifted, or exactly 0, which reads as +1.
- When a flank man swings, `ChooseLook` gives him the look as a threat. His raw yaw is past 100, so `HoldSide` returns `Side * 100` using the presser's residual sign. Nothing re-takes the side for a new man.

**The inputs are realistic**
- The crowd's own formation puts men at +-110 off the player's facing (`SaudBrain::RoleBearing`), 10 past `LookMaxYawDegrees`. The steer's 4-12 degree band keeps them mostly past 100.
- `IsBehind` is over 120, so flank men are not limited by the behind rule and do claim attack tokens.
- A thug's circling (0.34 x 288 cm/s, coin-flip direction) sets the residual's sign, so the outcome is a coin flip per swing.

**Visible**
- The player's own character twists his chest 35 degrees and his head 65 more over the shoulder opposite the man swinging at him, looking at nobody.
- It lasts 0.12-0.24 s per swing, then snaps back. The guard fists turn with the face.
- It is the same at 30, 60 and 120 Hz, and happens on roughly half the flank swings in any fight with two or more enemies.

**It breaks two stated promises**
- The header (SaudIK.h:1537): "Past the yaw limit the face holds the limit rather than turning away from the fight".
- CLAUDE.md, "Body and look": he looks at "a man swinging at him".

**Not a recorded decision**
- CLAUDE.md records only "the look holds one shoulder for a man straight behind".
- The existing test (`HoldSide(-120, Side = +1)` gives +100) covers one man already held over a shoulder crossing behind. It does not cover a side inherited from a different man half a degree off the front.
- Nothing in the four IK sections or "Known, not fixed" lists this.

**Check on the finder's probe**
It calls the real functions in `UpdateTurn`'s order. Its 40 cm/s drift is below the thug's real circling pace, and its numbers match mine to about a degree.

### Their fix advice
Leave `HoldSide` as it is, and re-take the side from the man only when the look is taken up afresh.

- **In `TurnStep`:** when the look is fresh and `|RawYaw| > LookMaxYawDegrees`, set `S.Side = RawYaw >= 0 ? 1 : -1` before the `HoldSide` call.
- **What counts as fresh:**
  - the target differs from last frame's — `UpdateTurn` knows this by comparing the pick to the previous `LookTarget`, and passes a flag;
  - the look was off last frame (`bMayLook` false or no target) — `TurnStep` can keep that itself as a bool on `FTurn`;
  - after the teleport reset.
- **Do not key it on the face's current angle.** At 120 Hz the first frame turns only 15 degrees, so a man straight behind swaying +-179 would flip the side every frame and the face would never commit. Identity of the look is the sound trigger.

It must not break:
- "a man straight behind: the face keeps one shoulder" — same target after the first frame, so the side is taken once and held;
- "...and changes it when he comes within the limit on the other side";
- "a look swung from one side to the other goes round the front, never behind";
- "a blow mid-turn never turns the face further from the facing" — reeling still gives Yaw 0;
- the 0.75 keep-share against two men trading the look;
- bites 311 and 312 in bites.txt, which must still be caught.

Add a test with a sabotage: settle on a man dead ahead with raw yaw +0.7, -0.7 and exactly 0, then switch to a new man at -110 and at +110. The face must end within 15 degrees of him at 30, 60 and 120 Hz.
