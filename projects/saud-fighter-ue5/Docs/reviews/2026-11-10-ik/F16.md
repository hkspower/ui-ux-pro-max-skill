## Finder's fix sketch
All inside FighterIK, no new Fighter field. (1) Strike(): on a swing's first frame take the limb's end as Guard()/SolveFoot left it this frame (transform.InverseTransformPoint(limb.End.position)) and blend from THAT instead of `rest`; come home to the guard/rest target of the frame. (2) A swing taken away: keep the last strike target and weight; when State leaves Attack before the row ends, let the weight go over a short ramp (Unreal's ContactTakenSeconds is 0.05 s on a limb the clip already carries; here the whole limb is the IK's, so about 0.1 s), not advanced while _fighter.Frozen -- so the contact frame really holds the limb. ### Their reasoning
All three parts reproduce on realistic inputs, and nothing records them as decided.

**Unreal has the behaviour (read line by line, then run).**
- `FRamp` (SaudIK.h:74) eases every share on a smooth step.
- `StepHolds` line 581 calls `LetGo` for a foot the strike takes; it is handed back over `ReleaseSeconds` 0.08.
- `FStrikeTrack` (1466-1507) lets a taken swing go over `ContactTakenSeconds` 0.05.
- The Unreal CLAUDE.md "Smoothness" paragraph says "Every share ramps on and off on a smooth step, never in a frame", with crossfades of 0.05 s into a strike and 0.03 s into a reel.

**Unity lacks it (FighterIK.cs).**
- `Strike()` line 599 blends from `EndRestLocal`, the A-pose, whatever `Guard()` did to the arm that frame.
- `Strike()` line 584 returns the moment State is not Attack.
- `HoldFoot` lines 263-267 returns `rest` at once for a striking leg.

**The inputs are ones the game makes.**
- `PlayerFighter.ReadInput` lets a punch start while block is held, and `StartAttack` clears Blocking.
- `Kick()` returns before any movement, so a kick out of a walk stops the root on that frame.
- `ReceiveHit` does not spare a man in Attack, so being hit mid-swing is an ordinary trade.

**A player would see it.**
- (c) is the strongest: a limb teleports 94-125 cm and the hit stop then holds that pose.
- (a) at 60 and 120 Hz is a backwards move of 49-67 cm, larger than any other frame of the punch. At 30 Hz it is hidden by the punch's own speed.
- (b) is a 16-19 cm average foot pop, up to 29-35 cm, where a standing kick moves 0.

**Not recorded as decided.** The Unity CLAUDE.md says only "the striking leg is not held"; Unreal says the same and still hands the foot back over 0.08 s. The code's own comments set the opposite standard: the guard moves "not in one frame or it reads as a glitch", and "the pair are frozen on the contact frame, hands included".

**The finder overstated two numbers.** Its reflection start skipped the swing's AttackTime-0 frame.
- (b) at 30 Hz: 30.7 / 43.4 cm reported, 15.9 / 29.3 measured. At 60 Hz: 22.9 / 37.0 reported, 19.5 / 33.7 measured.
- (a): 60.2 / 69.8 cm reported, 49.3 / 64.3 measured; its 30 Hz case is not a visible snap.
- (c) is exact.

The fix fits in FighterIK.cs with private state, as `_guard`, `_hipDrop` and `_holdL` already are, and is testable under Mono.

### Their fix advice
All inside FighterIK.cs, with no new Fighter field.

1. **`Strike()`: blend from where the limb is, not from the A-pose.** Take `from` as the limb's end as `Guard()`/`SolveFoot` left it this frame (`limb.End.position`, read before `Apply`) and use `Lerp(from, full, weight)`. With no guard and a standing foot, `from` equals `rest`, so the result must stay identical to today's: my "none" trails and tests/IK.cs's StrikeWeight/StrikeTarget/WindUp checks must not change.

2. **A swing taken away: ease it out.** Keep the last strike target, weight and limb in private fields. When State leaves Attack before the row's end, ease the weight to 0 over about 0.05-0.10 s on a smooth step, laid over the Guard/flinch target of the frame.
   - It must not advance while `_fighter.Frozen`, so the contact frame really holds the limb.
   - It must always finish, including for Down and Dead.
   - It must be cleared when a new swing starts.
   - It must do nothing at a swing's normal end, where the weight is already 0.

3. **`HoldFoot(striking)`: hand the foot back over time.** Move the place from its Anchor (or a step's current place) to `rest` across `ReleaseSeconds` 0.08 on a smooth step, by dt so it is the same at 30/60/120 Hz, and held through `frozen`.
   - Keep `Held` and `Stepping` false for a striking leg, so HipDrop's "striking leg does not count" and the other foot's `otherSteps` logic are unchanged.
   - Return `rest` exactly when the anchor is already at `rest`.
   - The one existing assertion that must be rewritten is Hold.cs line 73 ("the striking leg is not held" expects the place to equal `rest` on the first frame).

Must not break: Hold.cs's walking checks (0.000 cm slide), Pose.cs's flat-ground checks to the millimetre and the guard returning to rest, and the IKDebug view flags.
