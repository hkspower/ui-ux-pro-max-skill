With bHold kept, StepHolds already braces him back in two shuffle steps (12 cm one at a time, 30 cm both). ### Their reasoning
The finder's numbers reproduce exactly, but this is the behaviour the project decided, wrote down and tests for, so by the audit's rules it is not a finding.

- **CLAUDE.md, twice.** Line 3761-3762: "A block pushed sideways still glides (200 cm/s): a block does not step." Line 4533-4534: "pushed faster (a shove) it still glides, as a block does not step."
- **The code's own comments.** SaudIK.h:672-675 names this exact case: "A clip that does not step while the capsule moves -- the Block walked, a blow's push -- glides as it did, rather than tap-dance." SaudIK.h:692-697 says the same for speeds above BlockShuffleMax.
- **A test asserts it.** Tools/harness/tests/ik.cpp:2115-2117 checks "pushed faster it glides", and 2141-2143 checks "a block walked glides". The finder's fix would have to reverse those checks.
- **The finding concedes it.** Its scenario opens "Recorded as decided" and its fix sketch says "This reverses a recorded decision, so it is the author's call."

What is actually on screen is a guarding man knocked back 3.5-25 cm over 0.075-0.108 s in his block pose, with the feet travelling with the body. Nothing pops, bends the wrong way, goes NaN, or changes with frame rate beyond 0.5 cm.

One part of the measurement is not covered by the "shove glides" decision, and it is a separate engine-side matter rather than this finding. The Jab row (50.4 cm/s, under the 60 cm/s threshold) glides only because SaudMotionAnimInstance.cpp:905 passes `St == EFighterState::Block`. No assignment of that state exists anywhere under Source/SaudFighter (Idle, Walk, Attack, Hit, Dash, Down and Dead are the only ones assigned); a guard is the `bBlocking` flag. With the flag on, my probe shows the Jab held at 0.02 cm with 2 steps. The same holds for ZAYOS's Cross, which sits under his scaled threshold. I confirmed this by grep only, since the engine side cannot be compiled here. Every blow from the Knee up glides either way, which is the decided behaviour.

### Their fix advice
No fix under this audit: making a shoved guard hold and step reverses a decision recorded at CLAUDE.md 3761-3762 and 4533-4534, in SaudIK.h:672-675 and 692-697, and asserted by ik.cpp:2115-2117 and 2141-2143, so it is the author's call. If the author does ask for it, it must leave a guard walked on the stick above 60 cm/s gliding, and those ik.cpp checks would need rewriting, not deleting. The separate point that SaudMotionAnimInstance.cpp:905 passes `St == EFighterState::Block` (never assigned) where `Fighter->bBlocking` looks intended belongs to an engine-side finding, not this one.
