### Their reasoning
Reproduced, and it holds. Note on the label: it is filed as kind "gap", but it is not a Unity-parity gap. Unity's FighterIK.Guard() only raises the hands and has no blow-meeting cover, so nothing here belongs in FighterIK.cs. I judged it as a hole between what the Unreal build promises and what it does; if "gap" must mean the Unity-parity test, it does not pass that test.

(a) The inputs are the game's own. A blocking man plays his set's Block clip (SaudFeel returns EClip::Block), and the Kick's timings come from DT_Attacks.csv. My extraction of the five Block clips matches the authoring code to the hundredth of a centimetre. The probe follows UpdateBlock and the proxy step exactly: MarkPoint on spine_03, MeetPoint 9 cm toward the kicker's thigh, CoverSide, FBlockTrack, then Cover with bHigh false.

The cause is geometric. Every Block holds the fist at the forehead only 29 to 35 cm from the shoulder, so the upper arm points straight forward and the elbow already sits within 1.5 to 2.0 cm of the lowest point of its fist-fixed circle. With the fist kept, "down" does not exist. The kick's meeting point lies under the shoulder, about 11 cm off the circle's axis, so "toward it" becomes a saturated 15 cm swing across the chest: +13.3 cm toward his right for a kick landing on his left ribs, 1.2 cm down. On the player's own block the elbow ends further from the kicking leg's line for every kicker from 124 cm out (18.7 -> 19.9, AL-WAHSH 7.7 -> 12.2, AL-SAQR 12.5 -> 15.6); only at 90 cm is it 2 cm nearer. The swing is the same whatever the body blow.

(b) CLAUDE.md line 3774-3776 promises "A block meets the incoming blow: ... low, the elbow turns down onto it". A player sees the covering elbow tuck 13 cm inward, away from the kick, while the guarded foot stops in the air beside and below it. It is smooth, not a pop.

(c) None of the four IK sections records this as decided or as known and left. They only say the harness scenes are synthetic men.

One correction to the finder: the harness check would pass on the real Block joints too. It asks only that the elbow end 1 cm nearer the point (it ends 2.5 nearer), never that it go down. So the test is too weak, not just run on the wrong fixture; the fixture is indeed not the Block (29.8 cm upper arm, 43.2 cm forearm, elbow hanging 22 cm under the shoulder).

Not judged: the finder's side remark that BlockMaxShift should grow with the man. In the engine that depends on whether a man's size comes from the component's scale, which cannot be seen here.

### Their fix advice
Unreal only: SaudIK.h, Cover's low branch, plus tests/ik.cpp and bites.txt. Nothing for Unity.

Replace the fist-fixed circle with a swing of the whole covering arm about the shoulder. Turn U = Elbow - Shoulder toward W = Meet - Shoulder by at most the BlockMaxShift chord at the elbow (Most = 2*asin(BlockMaxShift / (2*|U|))), times Alpha. Carry the forearm and fist as one piece: Out.Mid = Shoulder + Swing(U, U, W, Share), Out.End = Shoulder + Swing(Hand - Shoulder, U, W, Share). Measured on the real clips, the elbow drops 12.4 to 13.3 cm and comes onto the kick's line (18.7 -> 5.7 cm), and the glove comes down 8 to 10 cm and forward, away from the face.

It must not break:
- **Head branch:** untouched.
- **No share:** Alpha 0 returns the clip exactly.
- **Lengths:** both segments kept (a rigid swing does this).
- **Travel:** the elbow moves at most 15 cm, and bites.txt lines 252 and 255 must still bite. Line 255's sed pattern targets the `const float Half = FMath::Min(1.f, BlockMaxShift / (2.f * R));` line, so keep that text or re-make the sabotage.
- **Gloves' gap:** the bare sketch brought the gloves to 8.9 cm (about 6.8 flat) on a Special against A_Street_Block. Cap the swing with the same Clear rule the head branch uses, so the glove keeps BlockFistGap * Scale from OtherHand.
- **Degenerate input:** return the clip when W is near zero or U is already along W; no NaN.
- **Frame rate:** it stays a pure function of Alpha. The sketch's steps equal today's (4.65 cm at 60 Hz).
- **Engine side:** leave it as it is. Place(CS, Arm, Ha, To, false) already takes a moved End. The upright glove then bends the wrist by the swing (about 26 degrees); making it rigid is an engine-file change that cannot be compiled here.

Rewrite the check "a body blow is met by the elbow on its circle, the fist kept up" on the real joints. A_Saud_Block key 0: shoulder (17.2, -18.0, 129.5), elbow (49.7, -9.5, 130.7), hand (33.3, -7.6, 151.3), other hand (33.3, 7.4, 151.3); the kick's meeting point is about (18.0, -12.2, 119.4). Ask that the elbow end at least 8 cm lower and no further from the blow's line than the clip had it; "1 cm nearer the point" passes today's fault. Repeat on ZAYOS's Block at 1.48.

If the fist must stay at the forehead instead, the only sound alternative is to refuse a turn that does not lower the elbow by a few cm and leave the clip alone. That removes the wrong-way swing but leaves "the elbow turns down onto it" undelivered, and CLAUDE.md's sentence would have to say so.
