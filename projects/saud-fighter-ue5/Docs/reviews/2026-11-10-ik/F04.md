### Their reasoning
The fault is real: I reproduced it with my own reader and probe, and the finder's figures match mine to the decimal on the 30-a-second time base.

- **Mechanism, read at SaudIK.h:1297.** The pole is the clip's own elbow direction, turned with the swing. In the real cross that direction turns about 120 degrees round the arm between keys 2 and 3 while the elbow's offset shrinks from 15 cm to 1.6 cm. That is invisible in the clip. The fold onto a close man's chin puts the elbow 19-29 cm off the line and takes the direction from that 1.6 cm.
- **Inputs are ones the game makes.** Nothing sets a capsule size, so two men can stand 68 cm apart (ACharacter's default radius of 34, from memory of the engine). The player walks at 341 cm/s into enemies who hold 105-125 cm only loosely. The cross and jab clips land unaided at about 100-105 cm, so every punch thrown closer folds. The gate stays at 1.00, the man is inside the blow box, and `hand_end_r` is parented to `hand_r`, so the engine's solve does run.
- **A player would see it.** At 60 Hz the upper arm swings 51 degrees in the single frame of contact, the elbow travels 27 cm (2.2 times the clip's fastest frame), and it ends above the fist and above his own eye line. The hit's 0.04 s freeze then holds that pose. It is present at 30, 60 and 120 Hz, on both possible time bases, for Saud and for a street man.
- **Not recorded as decided.** The four IK sections of CLAUDE.md and "Known, not fixed" say nothing about the folded elbow's direction. The only related line is that a pole on the limb's line keeps the clip's bend. The existing tests only use a fixture with the elbow well under the line.

One nuance against the finding's wording: on the 24-a-second time base the elbow is still under the fist on the first whole-alpha frame and goes over it two frames later, so there it is a three-frame swirl, not a one-frame flip.

### Their fix advice
Change only the pole in `SolveStrike`, and only for arms.

**The fix.** Trust the clip's bend direction by how far its elbow stands off the limb's line, and replace it only as far as the solve folds the limb:
- Trust = clamp(clip elbow offset / (0.25 × upper length)).
- Fold = clamp((clip reach − wanted reach) / (0.15 × limb length)).
- W = (1 − Trust) × Fold.
- Bend = normalize(clip bend × (1 − W) + natural × W), where natural is down and half outward with the along-limb part removed.
- Pole = Root + Bend × upper length.

Keep it a pure function of this frame's pose, with no smoothing state, so 30, 60 and 120 Hz agree.

**What the caller must pass.** `SolveStrike` needs Up (`Frame.Up`) and an outward direction. Derive outward from the pose, for example this shoulder minus the other shoulder, flattened. Do not hard-code a mesh axis: component space on an imported mesh is not X-forward.

**What it must not break.**
- Fold is 0 at alpha 0 and when the target is at the clip's own reach, so these checks stay bit-identical: "at no share, the clip's own limb", "a straight clip arm is left alone at no share", and "a strike swung to a target at its own reach turns as one piece" (`bites.txt` line 248).
- "The strike turns with the fight, 72 angles" holds as long as natural is built from Up and the limb's own side.
- Tip on target, segment lengths and "a rigid end carries its tip onto the target" are untouched, because only the pole changes.
- The kick and Special go through the same function with the ball as tip. A knee's natural bend is not down, so leave legs on the clip's bend (an arm flag, or no natural direction given for a leg).
- Mirror the outward direction for the jab's left arm.
