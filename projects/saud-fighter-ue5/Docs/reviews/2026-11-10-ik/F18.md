### Their reasoning
I could not refute it: the gap is real on all four tests for a "gap" finding.

**Unreal has it.** The saud-fighter-ue5 CLAUDE.md records both halves in "The in-game IK, improved": "A snapped facing turns the drawn body by the root and the hips come round after it (16/s, 18 degrees a frame at most)" and "Standing still, a foot a turn has left more than 3 cm off its spot steps back under him after a quarter second". It lists the old behaviour ("a snapped facing turned the whole mesh in a frame") under "What was wrong before". The code is SaudIK.h TurnStep/SettleCapped (lines 1613, 1665) and StepHolds' settle block (lines 594-609), called from SaudMotionAnimInstance.cpp UpdateTurn (line 1231) and `bSettleFeet = bStanding && !bAttacking` (line 902). My own probe of the real header reproduces 18 degrees a frame at 60 Hz and feet ending within 3 cm.

**Unity lacks it.** Fighter.FaceTowards (Fighter.cs line 598) sets `transform.rotation` at once and the Mesh child follows rigidly. FighterIK.HoldFoot has no settle: a held foot only moves past StepDrift (12 cm). An ankle 8.2 cm from his middle is carried 11.5 cm by a quarter turn, so it never steps.

**The inputs are ones the game makes.**
- PlayerFighter.ReadInput calls FaceTowards(position + wish) every frame the stick is pushed, on GetAxisRaw, so a reversed key is a half turn in one frame.
- Punch, Kick and ReleaseRage call FaceNearestOpponent first.
- Enemies call FaceTowards every frame (FightStyleRunner line 118, EnemyFighter TickAI line 232). This is the commonest case: an enemy turned through a quarter circle by a circling player is left with both ankles on his centreline.

**A player would see it.** The whole body turns 180 degrees in one frame, against 18 a frame in Unreal. The legs cross for about 0.1 s, with one foot stepping onto the other's spot. After a quarter turn he stands with one foot directly in front of the other for as long as he stands. The end state after a walk also depends on frame rate (11.3 / 0.0 / 2.8 cm at 30 / 60 / 120 Hz).

**Not recorded as decided.** The Unity CLAUDE.md's note on the foot hold lists what was ported (StepDrift, StepSeconds, ForceDrift, SnapDrift, hit stop) and says nothing about leaving out the settle or the turn's lag. The port's "frozen, not kept in step" status is lifted for IK by this task.

**It fits FighterIK.cs and the Mono harness.** FighterIK already owns the Mesh child (it resets `_mesh.localPosition` each frame). The stub Transform composes localRotation through parents. HoldFoot is pure and already executed by tests/Hold.cs.

The finder's numbers match mine apart from definitions. They measured from the hip joint (12.2 and 6.7 cm); I measured from the foot's rest place (11.5 and 6.3 cm). Their 0.367 s for Unreal's half turn is 0.375-0.400 s by my count.

### Their fix advice
Keep it all in FighterIK.cs, in two parts.

**1. The settle, in HoldFoot (pure).**
- Add `Still` and `LastDrift` to FootHold.
- While the foot is held, not frozen, the fighter is not attacking, and its drift has changed by less than about 0.05 cm x size since last frame, add dt to Still; otherwise zero it.
- When Still >= 0.25 s, drift > 0.03 m x size, and the other foot is held and not stepping, start the ordinary step and zero Still. Take the further foot first; that needs the other foot's drift passed in, or accept left-first.
- It needs a new "may settle" input (state not Attack), as Unreal's `bSettle = standing && !attacking`. The existing `striking` flag only covers the kicking leg.

**2. The turn lag.**
- Add a pure `TurnLag(current, target, dt, striking)`: `step = wrap(target - current) * (1 - exp(-16 * q * dt))`, clamped to +/- 1080 * q * dt, with q = 3 in an attack.
- Keep a drawn yaw on FighterIK, as `_hipDrop` is kept. Set it at once on the first frame and on a move over SnapDrift; hold it while Frozen.
- Key it on the root transform's yaw, not on Fighter.Facing. tests/Pose.cs's rig has Facing = +X with an identity rotation, and keying on Facing would turn that rig 90 degrees.
- Each LateUpdate, before FindFoot, set `_mesh.localRotation = AngleAxis(lag, up) * meshRestRotation` and turn `_meshRest` about the root's axis the same way. Store the Mesh's rest localRotation at Start; an imported model may not be identity.
- Take the feet's rest points and the knee poles from the drawn yaw: `transform.TransformPoint(AngleAxis(lag, up) * EndRestLocal)`, and the pole along the drawn forward. Otherwise the feet step under the root's facing while the hips still face the old way.
- Unlike the finder's sketch, the guard's targets must also ride the drawn yaw. Left on Fighter.Facing, a guard held through a half turn solves the fists 26 cm behind the still-unturned chest for about 0.2 s. Only the strike's target stays on Fighter.Facing, with the 3x quickening.

**Must not break.**
- tests/Hold.cs: walking at 1.0-2.5 m/s still slides 0.000 cm with no extra steps (the settle must not fire while the rest point moves); hit stop still holds a step; its nine sabotages still bite.
- tests/Pose.cs: flat ground stays a no-op to 2 mm from the first frame (the lag starts at zero); "after the root moves, the feet are still on the ground that frame"; the guard checks (hands in front along the facing, both the same height, back to rest).
- The hips' drop and the IKDebug view.
- Fighter.Facing, InHitbox and every strike target: the fight's own facing stays instant.

**Tests to add.**
- TurnLag pure: at most 36 / 18 / 9 deg a frame at 30 / 60 / 120 Hz; the same place within a degree at 0.2 s across rates; arrives; holds at dt 0.
- Pose-style: after FaceTowards 180 the shoulders' first frame is under 20 deg and the legs never cross; after 90 and 45 on the spot both ankles are within 3 cm of their places by 1 s.
