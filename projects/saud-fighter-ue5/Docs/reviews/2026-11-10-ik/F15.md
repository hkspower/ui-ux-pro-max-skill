## Finder's fix sketch
Take the man's size from his bones, once, in Start (DistrictRuntime.Spawn calls ConfigureFrom, and so SetStature, before the first Start): _size = hip joint's rest height in the root's frame / HipHeight (or the Mesh child's localScale.y against its value at bind). Use it for HoldFoot's size (so StepDrift, ForceDrift, SnapDrift and StepLift grow as documented); use each leg's own EndRestLocal.y in place of the AnkleHeight constant in FindFoot; multiply GuardHeight, GuardForward and GuardSpread by it in GuardTarget (an extra parameter keeps it pure); and use it for the debug view's HipsFrom. Leave the strike's landing height and reach alone -- they are the attack row's by decision ('Reach is the attack row's and does not grow').

### Their reasoning
Confirmed line by line and reproduced. EnemyFighter.SetStature (line 101) multiplies only the Mesh child's localScale (and the controller); Bootstrap.MakeBody leaves the root unscaled on purpose (its comment says so); FighterIK sits on the root and line 327 reads transform.localScale.y, so HoldFoot's size is 1 for every man. GuardTarget (line 458) and FindFoot (line 342) use fixed metres (1.50 / 0.26 / 0.11, ankle 0.070). FighterIK.Start runs after ConfigureFrom on both spawn paths, so the rest points are the scaled ones while the targets are Ahmed's.

Visible: the guard is the state the file itself says the player most needs to read off a body, and its constant is documented as 'up at the chin'; tests/IK.cs asserts 'guard sits above the shoulder'. That fails for every man above about 1.04: the Boss (styles.json guardChance 0.52) holds his fists 39 cm under his own chin at mid-torso, and ZAYOS (0.42, the cellar boss of area 1) brings them to his hip joints, lower than they hang. These are roster rows that the stage and strata waves really spawn.

Promise broken: HoldFoot's comment ('Size scales every distance with the man'), tests/Hold.cs ('a man half again Ahmed's size holds 15 cm (his StepDrift is 18)') and the port's CLAUDE.md ('distances grow with the root's scale') describe growth that never happens at runtime because the root's scale is always 1. The harness stub's own comment ('nothing in the port puts a scaled object above a bone') shows the blind spot.

Gap test: Unreal has it. SaudIK.h line 887 takes Size from the man's own leg length; HoldDrift, HoldDriftHard, HoldTeleport, StepLift, PlantAlpha and MaxPelvisDrop are multiplied by it; tests/ik.cpp BigMan() checks ZAYOS at 1.48; the block is the man's own clip and Cover uses BlockFistGap x Scale. Unity lacks it, as measured. The fix is local to FighterIK.cs and testable under Mono with a pre-scaled rig.

Not recorded as decided or left: neither CLAUDE.md mentions the guard's height against size; the only recorded decision nearby is that strike reach is the attack row's and does not grow, which the finding leaves alone. The one Unreal 'known, not fixed' entry about sizes is about DT_Fighters.csv, not this.

Corrections to the finder, none of which changes the verdict: (1) the 0.20 m step numbers (sole -3.8 cm under the step) are on a fixture the game's districts do not have -- Pose.cs itself says every district is one flat slab and paving is not solid -- so that part should not be counted; (2) 'the four biggest are the four with the highest guardChance' is slightly off: Saqr (1.14, 0.37) sits above Bouncer and Grappler; (3) the Runner's 0.42 cm float and 4.2 cm knee, and the unscaled step drift, are real but barely visible; the guard and the vanishing step lift (2.1 cm on a 2.8 m man) are what a player would see. The pre-scaled skeleton is an assumption about Unity's transform composition (a child's world position takes its parent's scale), which is standard Unity behaviour but has never been run in an editor here.

### Their fix advice
Smallest sound fix, all in FighterIK.cs:
1. In Start, after Bind, measure the man once from his bones: _size = the thigh joints' rest height in the root's frame (transform.InverseTransformPoint(leg.Root.position).y, averaged over the legs that bound) / HipHeight; fall back to 1 when no leg bound or the value is not finite or <= 0. Do not read Mesh.localScale for it: the FBX's import scale need not be 1, and SetStature has already multiplied into it by the time Start runs.
2. FindFoot: pass _size to HoldFoot in place of Mathf.Abs(transform.localScale.y), and use leg.EndRestLocal.y in place of the AnkleHeight constant for the target's height, so flat ground is a no-op for every size (this also removes the Runner's 0.42 cm float).
3. GuardTarget: add a size argument as an overload or a defaulted parameter (so tests/IK.cs lines 120-122 still compile and pass) that multiplies GuardHeight, GuardForward and GuardSpread; GuardHand passes _size. Optionally HipsFrom in the debug view uses HipHeight x _size.

Must not break: Ahmed and every scale-1 man stay exactly as now (Pose's millimetre ankle checks, guard at 1.50 +/- 0.05 and 'returns the hand to rest', Hold's 0.000 cm slide); HoldFoot's signature and its 'size 0 is taken as 1' rule (tests/Hold.cs); strike reach and the strike's landing heights stay the attack row's and the existing constants (the recorded decision in SetStature's comment); the root stays unscaled (the CharacterController depends on it); a limb that failed to bind still only disables itself; the port still compiles with warnings as errors. Start-time measurement relies on ConfigureFrom running before the first frame, which both DistrictRuntime.Spawn and WaveDirector do. In the harness the stub does not compose scale, so the new test must build its rig pre-scaled (bone positions x size) or the stub's Transform must first learn to compose a parent's scale; taking the size from the bones works under either. The CLAUDE.md line 'distances grow with the root's scale' should then say the man's size.
