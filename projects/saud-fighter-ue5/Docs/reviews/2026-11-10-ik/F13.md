## Finder's fix sketch
Let the hips come down for reach as well as for ground height. In LateUpdate, after both FindFoot calls and before the drop is applied, for each planted, non-striking foot compute need = (leg.Root.position.y - target.y) - sqrt(L*L - flat*flat), where L is 0.995 x (thigh + shin length, measured once in Bind) and flat is the horizontal distance from the hip joint (mesh at rest) to the foot's target; a foot with flat >= L asks for MaxHipDrop. want = max(HipDrop(...), needL, needR), capped at MaxHipDrop. With straight rest legs this formula already contains today's ground rule. Come down at once or fast (the foot has to reach this frame), rise through EaseDrop; hold through hit stop as now. At 34 cm carried that is 6.8 cm of drop, which reads as a walking bob. Keep the white hips line in IKDebug fed from the same number.

### Their reasoning
Reproduced with my own probe, and the numbers agree with the finder's to the millimetre (4.2 cm up, 1.4 cm off, 3.3-3.5 cm/m at 3.408 m/s 60 Hz; 14% of foot-frames on the floor).

Why it is real:
- Line by line: FighterIK.LateUpdate (lines 142-156) gives SolveFoot a target at HoldFoot's anchor on the floor. HipDrop (367-373) looks only at ground height, so on flat ground the hips never move (measured 0.00 cm). The leg rests at full extension (slack 0.00 mm on the Pose fixture, 0.33 mm with the generator's real knee). TwoBoneIK.Solve line 75 then straightens the leg toward a target it cannot reach, so the ankle swings on an arc about the hip.
- Inputs are the game's own: the Pose suite's joint table (checked against build_saud.py), speeds from fighters.json through Fighter.AddMovement, sizes 0.94-1.55 from the same file, 30/60/120 Hz. The finder's probe drives the real code the same way; I found nothing wrong with it.
- The promise is broken. HoldFoot's doc says "held, the foot stays where it was planted", FindFoot's says "keep the sole on whatever is under it", and the CLAUDE.md entry says "a held foot slides 0.000 cm". That figure comes from tests/Hold.cs, which measures HoldFoot's return value, not the bone. The debug view also draws its green "held" cross up to 4.4 cm from the ankle.
- Not recorded as decided or known. HipDrop's comment and the 2026-11-09 entry cover only ground lower than the root. Nothing in either CLAUDE.md or the tests mentions a foot out of reach sideways. The 2026-11-09 change treated the same symptom (a foot dangling at full extension) as a fault to fix.

Two qualifications:
- **Severity is medium, not high.** The follow camera sits 11 m back, so 4 cm is about 4 px at 1080p (more when a wall pulls the boom in). It is a held foot peeling 3-4.5 cm off the floor and creeping about 1.4 cm, with both ankles more than 1 cm up in 43% of frames at Ahmed's pace. It is not a pop or a snap.
- **Frame-rate difference is mostly sampling.** The maximum differs by Hz (2.76 / 4.21 / 4.21 cm at 3.408 m/s) because HoldFoot starts its step on the first frame past 12 or 30 cm. The fault is present at every rate.

### Their fix advice
Prototyped in <scratch>/ik/verify-unity-ik-0/fix/FighterIK.cs. Held ankles land 0.00 cm from their targets at every speed, rate and size tried, and Pose, Hold, IK and IKDebug pass against it.

Smallest sound fix, all in FighterIK.cs:
1. **Bind:** keep each leg's rest span |End - Root| and its standing height Root.y - End.y.
2. **New pure function:** ReachDrop(stand, span, flat, below) = clamp(below + stand - sqrt(span^2 - flat^2), 0, MaxHipDrop), returning MaxHipDrop when flat >= span.
3. **LateUpdate:** after both FindFoot calls, with the mesh still at rest, for each foot that found ground and is not the striking leg:
   - flat = horizontal distance from leg.Root.position to the foot's target;
   - below = (rootY + the leg's own rest ankle height) - target.y;
   - want = max(HipDrop(...), both needs), capped at MaxHipDrop.
4. **Timing:** apply the sideways part (need minus that foot's ground depth below the root) at once: _hipDrop = max(EaseDrop(_hipDrop, want, dt), atOnce). Leave the ground-height part on EaseDrop.

What it must not break:
- **Standing must stay a no-op.** Use the rest span, not the finder's 0.995 x (thigh + shin): that drops a standing man's hips 4.4 mm. This leaves only 0.6 mm inside Pose's 5 mm "hips are lower by the step" check and breaks FindFoot's "no-op to the millimetre".
- **Scaled men must not sink.** FindFoot's target uses the unscaled AnkleHeight (0.070), but EnemyFighter.SetStature scales the Mesh, so a big man's ankle rests at 0.070 x scale. Today the straight leg hides this. The finder's formula (hip.y - target.y) sinks a standing 1.26x man 1.82 cm and ZAYOS (1.55x) 3.85 cm. Use the leg's own standing height in the reach term, and the leg's own rest ankle height (leg.EndRestLocal.y) instead of AnkleHeight for the target in FindFoot. With both, 0.94x to 1.55x stand and walk at 0.00 cm.
- **A kerb must still ease.** Hold.cs checks the drop never moves more than 4.5 cm in a frame; only the sideways part may come down at once.
- **Existing rules stay:** first frame at once, held through Frozen, a foot over nothing and the striking leg do not count, never above rest, MaxHipDrop cap. IKDebug's white hips line already reads _hipDrop.

One thing for the fixer to judge: with at-once down and a 12-per-second rise, the hips ripple 2.1-4.7 cm about eight times a second at 3.408 m/s (1.7 cm in a frame at 60 Hz). That is HoldFoot's 0.14 s shuffle showing through. A slower rise for the reach part would flatten it; I did not test that.
