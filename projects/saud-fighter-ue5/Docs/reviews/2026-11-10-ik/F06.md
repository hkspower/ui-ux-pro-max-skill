# F06  [unreal / fault / high / confirmed]
A half-turn clip (Turn_180, Pivot_180) spins the drawn body the long way round through its 0.06 s cut when the stick is on the side away from the clip's way round

file: Source/SaudFighter/Combat/SaudIK.h line 1683
measured by the finder: True
finder's probe: <scratch>/ik/find-unreal-look/turn_cut.cpp

## Scenario
A free man (nobody within 12 m: the open world between fights, or an enemy beyond 12 m) standing still pushes the stick more than 135 degrees off his facing, or reverses at a run. SaudSteer::StepFree snaps the facing to the wish and plays Turn_180 / Pivot_180. That clip always starts turned back +180 and goes round one way (build_motion.py: TURN_DEG -180, th = +180*(1-ease)). TurnStep takes the lag it found the SHORT way (-150 for a wish of +150) and fades it to nothing as the clip's weight comes in, so for a wish on the + side the lag unwinds and the clip's turn-back winds in the same direction. Probe inputs: real StepFree, SaudFeel::CutBetween (0.06 s), FCrossfade, TurnStep; the clip's own yaw from build_motion.py's formula at 30 fps; the two poses mixed both as tests/ik.cpp mixes them (Actor + S.Hips + W*Clip) and as a normalised quaternion lerp; 30, 60, 120 Hz. A straight-back stick is +-180 by float noise (WrapDegrees(-180) is +180), so about half of those go the bad way too.

## Finder's fix sketch
TurnStep has to know the clip's own turn. Hand it the clip's signed start angle (and its body yaw now): when the clip is taken (S.bClip set), put the found lag on the clip's side of the circle (if ClipHips and the clip's start differ by more than 180, add or take 360 -- HipsKeepWay already allows a lag past a half turn), so the lag going out and the clip's turn-back coming in cancel instead of adding. tests/ik.cpp's own formula then gives 150 -> 210(1-W) + 177W, a 33 degree move the clip's own way.

## Finder's test sketch

### Their reasoning
I could not refute it; my own probe reproduces the finder's numbers to the decimal (325.0 degrees through the 0.06 s cut where 35.0 is needed; drawn pelvis +54, -165, -53, -35 on four successive 60 Hz frames) with stricter inputs than the finder used.

Checked line by line:
- SaudSteer::StepFree (SaudSteer.h 263-295): TurnOnSpot and Pivots take |Error| only, and Out.FacingYaw = Wrap180(Want), so a half turn is started with a snap of either sign. AFighterBase::SteerFree -> FaceYaw -> SetActorRotation applies it in one frame, for Saud (SaudCharacter.cpp:318) and for free enemies.
- There is one Turn_180 and one Pivot_180 per set (SaudFeel::TurnClip, no mirror). The shipped FBX files confirm what build_motion.py says: the pelvis starts 180.00 off the guard and comes round one way only (178.75, 175.02 ...), on the side Turn_R90 starts on (+90) and opposite Turn_L90 (-90).
- TurnStep (SaudIK.h 1676-1692): the lag is S.Hips - WrapDegrees(Turned), the short way, and `S.ClipHips = S.Hips` is faded out as ClipShare rises, whichever side the clip's turn-back is on. The proxy mixes guard and clip by BlendTwoPosesTogether, which takes the shortest arc toward the clip's ~179 degrees. For a snap on the clip's side the two cancel (stick -150: the body moves 25-38 degrees); for a snap on the other side they add (stick +150: 325).
- Order in NativeUpdateAnimation is PlayPicked, Fade.Advance, UpdateTurn, as both probes model it.

Visible: at 30 Hz the whole body is drawn 164.5 degrees from where it stood for one frame (a 195 degree turn in a frame), at 60 Hz three frames of spin, while the feet's anchors are held. The project's own standard is 18 degrees a frame for the hips, and tests/ik.cpp holds 'drawn where it stood through the cut' to 4 degrees -- but only for a snap of exactly +90 into Turn_L90.

Realistic: any standing about-turn while free with the stick on the clip's off side (half of them), and in my model 619 of 720 exact keyboard reversals (an exact half turn reads as +180 because WrapDegrees maps -180 to +180). The fault does not depend on how the FBX import signs the turn: with the other sign the sides exchange.

Not recorded: CLAUDE.md '-- 2026-10-04' says only that a turn clip 'carries the turn -- the hips' lag is taken out as it fades in, so the body turns once', and the TurnStep comment promises 'the drawn body stays where it stood through the cut'. Nothing lists the off-side half turn as known or left.

Two corrections to the finding's detail:
- Pivot_180 is not simply '+ bad, - good'. From Saud's motion-capture run it is (8 of 8 phases against 0 of 8). From Run_Fwd, which the street men and bosses play, the run's pelvis is within a few degrees of a half turn from the pivot's first frame, the blend's shortest arc flips with the stride's phase, and the body goes the long way in 4-5 of 8 phases on either side.
- The straight-back case is worse than 'about half' in my model: 619 of 720.

Seen on the way, outside this finding and not IK, not verified beyond the file headers: the men's clip FBX files (Saud, Street) are written on a 24 fps time base (GlobalSettings TimeMode 11, keys 1/24 s apart; A_Saud_Turn_180 spans 0.833 s against the table's 0.700, A_Saud_Jab 0.292 against 0.260), while the Island creatures' are 30 fps. build_motion.py never sets the scene's frame rate; build_primate_motion.py does.

### Their fix advice
Smallest sound fix, tried on a scratch copy of SaudIK.h (not on the tree): give TurnStep one more trailing parameter, the clip's own signed turn-back at its first frame, defaulting to 0 (today's behaviour, so every existing call and test is unchanged): -90 for Turn_L90, +90 for Turn_R90, +180 for Turn_180 and Pivot_180 (minus build_motion.TURN_DEG). The anim instance can keep it beside ClipTurns when Play is called with bTurn. On the frame the clip is taken (`!S.bClip`), if S.Hips is more than 180 from that angle add or take 360, then `S.ClipHips = S.Hips` as now. Move S.Hips itself, before `const float Hips = S.Hips`, not only ClipHips: otherwise the chest and face settle from the other branch and jump by a whole turn against the hips. Measured with this: stick +150 turns 35.0 through the cut (needs 35.0), most in a frame 20.6 / 13.5 / 9.5 at 30 / 60 / 120 Hz; the keyboard about-turn never goes round (0 of 720); Saud's pivot from his motion-capture run is right on both sides.

It must not break:
- the exact quarter turn (snap +90 into Turn_L90 still under 4 degrees through the cut; the lag and the turn-back are already on one side, nothing moves);
- HipsKeepWay: the moved lag is at most 225 (a wish of 135), under 270, so the next frame's keep-way wrap does not fire;
- S.Hips exactly 0 once the clip's weight is 1, and bClip cleared with nothing left to settle when the clip is no longer newest;
- the teleport reset;
- GetShownFacing and the debug line, which will read a lag past 180 for up to 0.06 s.

What it does not close: Pivot_180 cut in from Run_Fwd (street men, bosses) still goes the long way in 4 of 8 stride phases on either side (336.7-398.1 degrees), because there the pose blend itself picks the arc. That needs the proxy to turn the layers under an incoming turn clip by the clip's turn-back before the fold (the lag is then only the short residual between the snap and the turn-back), or a whole cut (0 s) for the half-turn clips. That is engine-side code nothing here can compile.

The new test should run StepFree's own snaps (+136, +150, +179, -179, -150, -136, and an exact reversal) into Turn_180 and Pivot_180 at 30 / 60 / 120 Hz, mix the two poses as a shortest-arc quaternion blend rather than `W * Clip`, and hold the turn through the cut to the gap between where he stood and the clip's own pose plus a few degrees, and no frame over about 30. Sabotage: today's `S.ClipHips = S.Hips` with no side taken fails at +150 with 325.
