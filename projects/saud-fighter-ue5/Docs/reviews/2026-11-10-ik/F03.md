A_Zayos_Jab / Cross / Hook are authored on a Saud-proportioned rig to land at Saud's chin height at clip scale (build_motion.py says so: his size is "actor scale in the engine"). **On the "gap" label.** The finding is tagged kind "gap" but is an Unreal promise-versus-behaviour hole in SaudIK.h, not a Unity parity gap. I judged it on the fault criteria; the Unity clause does not apply to it.
