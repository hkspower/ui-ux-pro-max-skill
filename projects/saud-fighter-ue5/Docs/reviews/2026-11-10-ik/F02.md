### Their reasoning
Confirmed line by line and reproduced with my own probe.

**The reading.**
- SaudMotionAnimInstance.cpp:904-905 passes `St == EFighterState::Block` as HoldsFeetMeasured's bBlocking.
- Every assignment to State under Source/ is Idle, Walk, Attack, Hit, Dash, Down or Dead (EnemyFighter.cpp:237/241, FightStyleComponent.cpp:327/453, SaudCharacter.cpp:296/339/343/633, FighterBase.cpp:241/254/327/341/394/432/468/561/606/627/686). None assigns Block.
- State is BlueprintReadOnly (FighterBase.h:227-228) and the tree has no .uasset, so nothing else can set it.
- A guard is the bBlocking flag over Idle or Walk. The same file reads it that way at line 1092 (`Fighter->bBlocking || St == Block`), as does SaudFeel::Pick (SaudFeel.h:682).
- So the fourth argument is always false. Pick plays A_<Set>_Block (stride 0), and any guard moving at 40 cm/s or more gets bHoldFeet = false: both feet are let go and travel with the capsule.

**The promise it breaks.** SaudIK.h:692-709 and CLAUDE.md "The IK, polished -- 2026-10-03" say a block at or under 60 cm/s (times his size) "now holds its feet and steps after him ... The anim instance passes the block state". CLAUDE.md records the opposite of this fault as done; nothing records it as known or left. Only the harness, which passes `true` by hand (tests/ik.cpp:2115-2121), ever runs the rule.

**The callers do produce it.**
- The player holds guard on a shoulder button and walks at 0.38 x 341 x stick tilt (SaudCharacter.cpp:307/337). A pad tilt of about 0.31-0.46 is 40-60 cm/s. The keyboard's 129.6 cm/s glides, as decided.
- A guarding enemy steps at 0.45 x |Step| x MoveSpeed (FightStyleComponent.cpp:435-450): the Thug circling is 44.1 cm/s, ZAYOS role-steering 52.4.
- Every blocked Jab pushes at 168 x 0.30 = 50.4 cm/s (FighterBase.cpp:596).

**On screen.** A man in his block pose skating with both feet flat on the floor: 82-115 cm in 2 s for the player, 23-27 cm per guard spell for an enemy, about 3.3 cm on each blocked jab. With the promised rule the same cases slide 0-5 cm at 60 and 120 Hz (up to 7.4 cm at 30 Hz) and step.

**Checked against the finder.** Its inputs are the real guard joints and Block rows, and its speeds are ones the game makes. My numbers agree with its on every case but one: 82 cm slid at 41 cm/s, 0 steps as written against 9 with the guard passed. The exception is its 6.22 cm for ZAYOS blocking a Cross, which is more than the Falling-then-braking model it states can push him (about 5.5-5.8 cm; mine 5.76 pushed, 5.65 slid) — immaterial to the verdict. Its 3.65 cm jab push against my 3.36 is a floor-gap modelling difference only.

**On the label.** The finding is tagged kind "gap" but is an Unreal-build hole between promise and wiring, not a Unity-parity gap. The Unity conditions do not apply to it and I judged it on the fault criteria.

### Their fix advice
Smallest sound fix: at SaudMotionAnimInstance.cpp:905 pass the guard as the Block clip is actually picked, not the bare state:

`const bool bGuardUp = (Fighter->bBlocking || St == EFighterState::Block) && (St == EFighterState::Idle || St == EFighterState::Walk || St == EFighterState::Block);`

This is the same expression UpdateBlock uses at lines 1092-1093. Pass `bGuardUp` as the fourth argument. The Idle/Walk gate matters: a man still holding the button while he reels (State Hit, a Hit clip playing) must not pick up the block clause.

Better still, put that one expression in an engine-free helper (for example in SaudFeel.h, over the int state and the flag) and use it in Pick, UpdateFeet and UpdateBlock, so the harness can test it. Add a check that it is true for (Idle, blocking) and (Walk, blocking) and false for (Idle, not blocking) and (Hit, blocking). If the harness reads source text, add a check that the HoldsFeetMeasured call no longer passes `St == EFighterState::Block` alone, with a sabotage for each.

It must not break:
- **The decided glides.** A guard faster than BlockShuffleMax x size still glides (the full-stick 129.6 cm/s guard walk; a blocked Cross, Hook or Kick on a Saud-sized man).
- **Everything else that holds or glides today.** A non-guarding man on a non-striding clip still glides; a walk's hold by stride, the standing hold and the attack hold are unchanged.
- **HoldsFeetMeasured itself.** Keep its signature and defaults: plant_dump.cpp:181 and feel.cpp:826 call it with three arguments, and ik.cpp:2114-2143, 2279-2281 and bites.txt 424/425/430 depend on it.

One visible consequence to expect: a blocked Jab's 3.36 cm push is over SettleDrift (3 cm x size) for men up to about 1.1x, so each foot takes one settle step about a quarter second later. That is the documented "holds its feet and steps after him", but it should be looked at when first played.

The .cpp cannot be compiled here, so the change is read-only reviewed. Keep it to that one expression.
