A quarter turn on the spot at any stick angle but 90: the body is jerked to the clip's 90 through the 0.06 s cut, the wrong way first when the stick is under 90

## Scenario
A free man standing still; the stick 46 to 135 degrees off his facing (a pad's stick gives any angle; a keyboard's back-diagonal gives 135; an enemy's wish is any angle). SaudSteer::StepFree snaps the facing to the WISH (Out.FacingYaw = Want) but TurnOnSpot plays Turn_L90 / Turn_R90, whose body starts turned back exactly 90. TurnStep fades the lag it found (the wish's angle) to nothing as the clip comes in, so the body is taken from where he stood to the clip's 90 inside the cut. tests/ik.cpp only tries a snap of exactly 90 and from it promises 'the body is drawn where it stood through the cut' and 'comes round once, never back'; TurnStep's comment and SaudFeel::CutIntoTurn say the same. Probe: real StepFree, CutBetween, FCrossfade, TurnStep; the clip's yaw from build_motion.py (th = -90*(1-ease(f/11)), 30 fps); 30, 60, 120 Hz; both pose mixes.

## Finder's fix sketch
Keep what the clip does not carry as lag instead of dropping it. With the clip's start angle C0 and its remaining share k of its own turn (1 at its first frame, 0 when its turn is done) handed to TurnStep: Residual = ClipHips - C0 (+40 for a 50 degree snap under Turn_L90), and under the clip S.Hips = ClipHips*(1-ClipShare) + ClipShare*Residual*k. The body then stays where he stood through the cut and the clip's 90 is drawn as the snap's own angle. (Or have StepFree snap the facing by the clip's angle and steer the rest at FreeTurnRate.)

### Their reasoning
Reproduced with my own probe; the numbers agree with the finder's to within the pose-mix difference (34.05 against 34.0 at 50 degrees, 50.95 against 51.0 at 135).

**Mechanism, confirmed line by line.**
- SaudSteer.h:289 snaps the facing to the wish (`Out.FacingYaw = Wrap180(Want)`), for any error from 45 to 135, and names Turn_L90 or Turn_R90.
- The clip's body starts exactly 90 back from the root (build_motion.py author_turn; turn_check holds it within 5 degrees).
- SaudIK.h:1692 fades the whole found lag (minus the snap) to nothing as the clip's weight comes in.
- So at the end of the 0.06 s cut the pelvis sits at snap minus about 84, not where he stood.

**The inputs are the callers' own.**
- ASaudCharacter::Tick passes a camera-relative pad stick against any facing to SteerFree; tests/steer.cpp itself asserts the snap-to-wish with L90 at every 5 degrees.
- EnemyFighter and FightStyleComponent steer the same way when free.
- The clip is picked the same frame (PlayPicked, CutIntoTurn 0.06), and `NewestTurns()` hands TurnStep the newest weight.
- Nothing else compensates: the posture twist is off under a turn clip (`bPostureTwist ... && !NewestTurns()`), and the chest and head are computed off the lag only, so they go with the pelvis.

**Visible.** On the player's own character in free roam, a pad push under 90 off turns the body 24-38 degrees the wrong way in 2-4 frames (up to 16-25 degrees in one frame) and then back the other way about 84. Over 90 it is jerked up to 51 degrees inside the cut. A pad almost never gives exactly 90, so most quarter turns show it.

**Broken promise.** TurnStep's comment ("the drawn body stays where it stood through the cut"), SaudFeel.h:784 and the two ik.cpp checks hold only at exactly 90, the single case tested.

**Not recorded.** The CLAUDE.md sections I read (Full IK at runtime; The in-game IK, improved; The IK, polished; Walking and running all 360 degrees; Posture and alignment; The IK, drawn in the game; Known, not fixed) say only "the hips' lag is taken out as it fades in, so the body turns once". They list 45 and 135 as judgement thresholds, and nowhere record the mismatch between the snap and the clip's angle as decided or left.

**Limit of the proof.** The engine side is read, not compiled, as everything in this build. The pelvis yaw is composed as lag plus mixed clip yaw, which is what FSaudMotionProxy::Evaluate does (clips blended, then the root turned by HipsYaw).

### Their fix advice
**Fix, in SaudIK::TurnStep (SaudIK.h:1683-1692).** Keep the part of the found lag the clip does not carry, tied to the clip's own progress.
- Add two defaulted arguments so the existing callers and tests compile unchanged: `ClipStartYaw` (the clip's own first-frame body yaw off the root: Turn_L90 -90, Turn_R90 +90, Turn_180 and Pivot_180 +180, from build_motion.py's TURN_DEG) and `ClipLeft` (the share of the clip's own turn still to come: 1 at its first frame, 0 when its turn is done).
- When the clip is first seen: `Residual = WrapDegrees(ClipHips - ClipStartYaw)`.
- Under the clip: `S.Hips = ClipHips * (1 - ClipShare) + ClipShare * Residual * ClipLeft`.
- The drawn body is then lag plus clip, which stays where he stood through the cut and comes round once by the snap's own angle (50 or 135), ending on the wish.
- UpdateTurn passes both values from the newest layer. `ClipLeft` is build_motion.py's `1 - ease(min(1, frame / done))` with done = N - 4 frames for the turns and N - 2 for the pivot. `SaudFeel::Ease` is already that easeInOutQuad. Hold the done-frames constant to the header from build_motion.py's check(), as TurnSeconds already is.
- The creatures' turns (build_primate_motion.py) have their own schedule: verify their curve, or measure a per-clip yaw into a generated header as SaudPlants.h does.

**Do not fix it in StepFree.** Snapping the facing by the clip's angle changes the combat facing and steer.cpp's "the facing snaps to the wish", and makes a 50-degree wish overshoot by 40 and come back.

**Must not break:**
- A snap of exactly 90 (Residual 0) gives today's numbers: within 4 degrees through the cut at 30 and 60 Hz, End == 90, and `S.Hips` exactly 0 once the clip's turn is done and once it stops being newest ("the clip over, nothing is left to settle").
- The "held at none" check needs rewording to "held at the residual times what is left", not deleting.
- A set whose turn falls back to the guard (TurnClip < 0) keeps the plain SettleCapped lag.
- Teleport reset and the HipsKeepWay wrap. For Turn_180 and Pivot_180 choose the residual's wrap so the body never travels against the clip's own way (the clip always goes to his right; a wish either side of 180 flips the snap's sign).
- The chest and head lines keep reading the pre-fade `Hips`.
- `GetShownFacing` (LastYaw + Turn.Hips) still feeds Pick's octant sensibly at the end of the turn.
- The feet keep their world anchors under a turn clip (ik.cpp:2430).
- bites.txt:567 still bites.
- The result is the same at 30, 60 and 120 Hz.

**Tests to add.** Run the ik.cpp block over snaps 46, 50, 60, 75, 90, 105, 120, 135 and their mirrors, with a sabotage that restores today's line 1692. The engine-side change (UpdateTurn) cannot be compiled here, so keep it to passing the two numbers.
