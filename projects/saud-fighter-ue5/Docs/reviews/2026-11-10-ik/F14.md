## Finder's fix sketch
Blend the strike from where the limb IS, not from its rest point. In Strike() take from = limb.End.position (it runs after the feet and Guard() have been solved this frame) and use Lerp(from, full, weight); at the end of the recovery the weight returns it to the live guard or rest with no step. For the leg, HoldFoot's striking branch should keep the foot's present place for the strike to start from (hold the anchor without stepping while striking, and let the ordinary step take it home afterwards) instead of returning rest at once. For a strike that ends early, remember the last strike target, limb and weight, and when State leaves Attack with weight > 0 let that weight run down over about 0.06 s (not ticking while Frozen, so the contact frame is held as it was struck) and keep solving Lerp(End.position, lastTarget, Smooth(w)). Feed IKDebug's strike cross from the same target.

### Their reasoning
The finding is real: I reproduced all three parts with my own probe, and the numbers match the finder's.

**(a) Punch out of the guard — the core, and solid.** Strike() line 598-599 blends Lerp(rest, full, weight) from the A-pose hand point. On the first frame with weight above 0, the fist is thrown from the chin to beside the hip while the guard weight is still 0.70-0.85, then reverses into the punch. The jump grows as the frame shortens (49.3 cm at 60 Hz, 64.3 cm at 120 Hz, 72.8 cm in the limit), so it is a discontinuity, not speed. At 30 Hz it is hidden inside the punch's own speed.
- The inputs are ones the game produces. PlayerFighter.ReadInput sets Blocking, then Punch() calls StartAttack, which clears it. FightStyleRunner runs TickDefence then TickOffence in the same frame, and EnemyFighter.TickAI does the same.
- The file's own words are broken. The comment at line 158-160 says the strike "re-solves from wherever the guard left the arm, so the two compose rather than fight". GuardBlend's comment says "not in one frame or it reads as a glitch".

**(b) Strike cut short by a blow — reproduced, but the weakest as a fault.** Through the real ReceiveHit, the striking limb goes 93.5-124.7 cm on the frame of the blow and he hangs there through hit stop. The designed flinch is 8.5 cm. A hit reaction cutting a pose is conventional. Still, the limb has already left by the "contact frame" that the comment at line 161-162 says is frozen, hands included.

**(c) Kick's first frame — reproduced, with a caveat on the fix.** The foot jumps 29-34 cm at worst, two to five times the walk's own fastest frame at 60 and 120 Hz. The mechanism is HoldFoot's striking branch returning the rest point at once.
- The Unity CLAUDE.md records "the striking leg is not held", and tests/Hold.cs line 73 asserts the rest point comes back at once. That letting-go is decided.
- The one-frame jump of the drawn foot is not recorded anywhere. So I do not count the fault as a recorded decision, but the finder's fix sketch for (c) ("hold the anchor without stepping while striking") would contradict the record and must not be used.

**Not recorded elsewhere.** Nothing in the Unity CLAUDE.md or the four Unreal IK sections records (a) or (b) as decided or left. The Unreal sections set the opposite standard for that build: "Every share ramps on and off on a smooth step, never in a frame".

**Limits.**
- Nothing has run in Unity; this is the harness stub.
- Which side the _l bones import on cannot be settled here. Both sides reproduce, and the uncrossed case gives the smaller numbers.
- Medium severity is fair.

### Their fix advice
All three fixes fit inside FighterIK.cs. I prototyped them in a scratch copy (verify-unity-ik-1/fix/FighterIK.cs); the measured results are in the numbers field.

1. **Punch out of the guard.** In Strike(), blend from where the limb is this frame, not from its rest point: `Vector3 from = limb.End.position;` then `Lerp(from, full, weight)`. One line. It runs after the feet and Guard() are solved, so it also starts from the hip-dropped hand on uneven ground.

2. **Strike cut short.** Keep the memory in FighterIK's own private fields: which limb, the last solved end in the fighter's frame (InverseTransformPoint), and a share.
   - Set them at the end of Strike() on every frame it solves.
   - When State is not Attack, run the share down over about 0.06 s, not while Frozen, and after Guard() solve Lerp(End.position, last, Smooth(share)) with the same pole rule.
   - Clear it when a new strike begins at weight 0.

3. **Kick's first frame.** Leave HoldFoot alone. In FindFoot, for a striking leg, ease the place (and the lift) from where that foot was put last frame to the rest point over the row's own startup: Smooth(AttackTime / startup). That needs last frame's place and lift per foot, as private fields.

What it must not break:
- **HoldFoot's striking contract.** tests/Hold.cs line 73 and the Unity CLAUDE.md's "the striking leg is not held" stand: rest returned, Held and Stepping false. Do not use the finder's "hold the anchor while striking".
- **Hit stop.** It holds everything; no run-down may tick while Frozen.
- **No new field on Fighter.** The Unity CLAUDE.md says the pose reads only state the fight already keeps.
- **The pure functions.** StrikeWeight, WindUp, StrikeTarget and tests/IK.cs stay untouched; at full weight the limb still points at the row's reach.
- **The punch from hands down.** It must stay the same to the digit (it does in the prototype).
- **The other hand and the flinch.** The hand not throwing stays in the guard, and the flinch on a struck man still shows (8.5 cm).
- **The debug view.** IKDebug's strike cross keeps being fed from the solved target.
- **The suites.** Hold, Pose, IK and IKDebug pass against the prototype and it builds with -warnaserror.
