### Their reasoning
Reproduced exactly; the finder's figures (25.03 / 15.41 at a quarter turn, 35.00 / 76.66 at a half turn, 20.76 / 12.18 at 30 Hz, 26.19 / 16.99 at 120 Hz) are what my independent probe prints.

Why it happens (SaudIK.h 1690-1701): under a turn clip S.Hips is cut to ClipHips*(1-ClipShare) in 0.06 s, but Chest and Head are still settled as `Hips + S.Chest` toward the facing at 22/s and 30/s and then stored as `- S.Hips`. The lag's fade is treated as the hips coming round 90 degrees in three frames, so the chest and face "fall behind" them. The drawn pelvis has not moved (the clip's body is turned back by what the lag gave up), so the stored offset is drawn as a twist the wrong way.

The inputs are ones the game makes:
- AFighterBase::SteerFree -> StepFree snaps the actor's yaw to the wish on the frame a standing turn starts (FaceYaw -> SetActorRotation).
- SaudFeel::Pick returns the turn clip in Idle or Walk; A_Saud_Turn_L90/R90/180 are on disk, so bOwnTurn is true for Saud.
- PlayPicked runs inside NativeUpdateAnimation before Fade.Advance and UpdateTurn, with CutIntoTurn 0.06 s.
- A free man has no look target (free is nobody within 1100-1200 cm, the look reaches 900), so Yaw is 0.
- The keyboard gives exactly 90 degrees; other stick angles show the same fault over the hips.

The engine side applies it: Frame.ChainYaw is put on spine_01-03, neck_01 and head over the blended clip pose (SaudMotionAnimInstance.cpp 257-275). The posture stage corrects only rolls and the hips-to-feet twist, and that twist is off under a turn clip, so nothing takes it back. The guard fists are carried with the face's turn as well.

A player sees it:
- On every quarter turn on the spot the shoulders jerk 22 degrees to the wrong side of where he stood and back within about 0.1 s.
- On a half turn the trunk sits at its 35-degree limit for five frames and the face whips 78 degrees the wrong way, 52 degrees in a single 60 Hz frame. The header says no 60 Hz frame turns the face over 30.
- It depends on frame rate: the face goes 55 degrees wrong in the world at 30 Hz against 78 at 60 and 120.
- The same push on the lag path leads the right way.

It is also a hole in the promise: FTurn says "a snapped yaw leaves them where they are in the world", and the rates' comment says "the face first, the chest after it, the hips last".

Not recorded: the four IK sections and "Walking and running all 360 degrees" say only that the hips' lag is taken out as the clip fades in. Nothing mentions the chest or face under a clip, and tests/ik.cpp's turn-clip block checks only the pelvis.

Caveat on the half-turn rows: they use the hand where the pelvis is drawn right (snap read as -179.99). The quarter-turn rows need no such choice.

### Their fix advice
Smallest sound fix: two lines in TurnStep (SaudIK.h 1700-1701), no new input and no engine-side change. While S.bClip, settle the chest and face over the hips rather than through the faded lag:

`const float Was = S.bClip ? 0.f : Hips, Now = S.bClip ? 0.f : S.Hips;`
`S.Chest = SettleCapped(Was + S.Chest, ChestLookShare * Yaw, ChestR * Q, ChestCap * Q, Dt) - Now;`
`S.Head = SettleCapped(Was + S.Head, Yaw, HeadRate * Q, HeadMaxTurn * Q, Dt) - Now;`

Under a clip the chain then keeps its place over the hips the clip draws and eases to its aim over them; with nobody to look at it adds nothing.

Tried on a scratch copy: 0.00 against the turn at 30, 60 and 120 Hz for sticks ±90, -179.99, 50, 60, 120 and -150, and the real tests/ik.cpp passes whole against it.

It must not break:
- The lag path (TurnClip -1) stays bit-identical: chest and face lead, a snapped yaw leaves them where they are in the world, 30/60 Hz agreement, HipsKeepWay, striking, lying and teleport.
- The turn-clip block of ik.cpp: the pelvis drawn where it stood through the cut (within 4 degrees), S.Hips held at 0 under the clip, "the clip over, nothing is left to settle", and bites.txt lines 566-567.
- Continuity on the frames bClip turns on and off: S.Chest and S.Head carry over unchanged. At the end S.Hips is 0, so both forms agree.
- GetShownFacing, which reads Turn.Hips; the fix does not touch it.

Do not use S.ClipHips as a fixed reference instead. It leads correctly through the cut but leaves the chest 35 and the face 90 degrees past the facing once the clip has brought the hips round.

The finder's version (measure from S.Hips + ClipShare * ClipYaw) would give a true lead under the clip. It needs the clip's own yaw passed from the anim instance, which nothing here can compile. Take it only if the other turn-clip findings add that input anyway.

Test to add in the turn-clip block: through Turn_L90 (snap 90) and Turn_180 (snap -179.99) at 30, 60 and 120 Hz, ShareTurn's trunk yaw (Yaw[0..2]) and face yaw (Yaw[0..4]) are never more than 2 degrees against the turn. Sabotage with today's two lines: it fails at 25.0 / 15.4 and 35.0 / 76.7.
