### Their reasoning
I tried to refute this and could not; the fault is real, with one caveat about the engine side.

**The code, line by line (SaudMotionAnimInstance.cpp):**
- Line 304 reads `CS.GetComponentSpaceTransform(Arm[S])`, where `Arm` is upperarm_l/r.
- Nothing before stage 2b reads a clavicle or anything under it: stage 1 touches the pelvis only, stage 2 the five chain bones, and `Raw` is a separate pose.
- Lines 321-323 hand `TurnBones` ten bones: the six turned bones, the two thighs, the two upper arms. No clavicle.
- `TurnBones` (530-577) sets only what is in `Read`.
- The skeleton is spine_03 > clavicle > upperarm, confirmed from Saud.gltf's node tree.

**Why the clavicle is left behind:** in the engine's `FCSPose`, a pose is one array of slots. Reading a bone converts it and every unconverted ancestor in place and flags them. So the upper-arm read converts the clavicle at its pre-posture place. `TurnBones` then turns spine_03 above it and the upper arm below it. At line 469, `ConvertComponentPosesToLocalPoses` makes each flagged bone relative to its parent's slot, which preserves the clavicle's stale component-space transform. The file's own comments (320, 390, 420-422) and CLAUDE.md line 1391 already work from this model; they simply missed the implicitly converted ancestor.

**The broken promise:** the header (SaudMotionAnimInstance.h 167-170) says only the bones in `Read` are set and "every other bone follows by itself". The comment at line 320 lists "the six, the thighs, the upper arms". The clavicles are converted, not in `Read`, and do not follow.

**The scenario is one the game produces:**
- `UpdatePosture` turns the gate on in Idle and Walk; `bPostureStraight` defaults to true.
- Gait is 1 for `_Mocap_` clips, which Saud plays whenever no man is within 12 m.
- The feet are wanted on the ground while walking.
- CLAUDE.md itself records the stage correcting 4.6-8.3 degrees of hip roll on these gaits.

**A player could see it:** the tee's yoke, shoulder tops and collar are skinned to the clavicles. They stay with the raw clip while the chest, neck and arms are straightened: 1.3-2.4 cm on average and up to 4.1 cm, swinging every stride. At the 5.6-7.4 m camera that is my estimate of roughly 3-6 pixels at 1080p. The visible shoulder line also keeps the roll the stage claims to level. I rate it medium-low: a smooth shear, not a tear, and only 0.4-1.6 cm in the guard-based look-aside case I tried (other men's walks are not in the data here).

**Not recorded as decided or known:** the four IK sections and "Posture and alignment" say nothing of the clavicles. Line 1391 only says the code "orders around" bones not following, which is the promise this breaks.

**Caveat:** nothing here compiles the engine. The `FCSPose` behaviour is from the engine's BonePose.h as I remember it (`CalculateComponentSpaceTransform` converting and flagging ancestors in place). I could not fetch the header to confirm: noahzuo.github.io and dev.epicgames.com are outside this session's allowed network domains. The single-array design leaves no other way for a child's read to work, so I am confident. `tests/posture.cpp` cannot see the fault: its `EngineTurn` assumes clavicles and upper arms all follow.

### Their fix advice
**Smallest sound fix:** in stage 2b (SaudMotionAnimInstance.cpp 321-323), hand `TurnBones` every bone the stage's reads converted, not only the ones it names. In practice that means adding the two clavicles, making `Read` twelve bones.

Better, find them rather than name them: for each upper arm, walk `Bones.GetParentBoneIndex` upward until a `Turned` bone is reached, and add every bone passed on the way. That is clavicle_l/r on this skeleton and still correct on one with an extra bone between.

`TurnBones` needs no change:
- it already reads every listed bone before setting any;
- it sorts parents first;
- it carries any bone under a turned one;
- it skips invalid indices;
- its inline allocator holds 16.

My control with the clavicles added printed 0.00000 cm.

**What it must not break:**
- The upper arms and thighs must still land exactly where `SaudIK::TurnJoints` puts the shoulders and hips (0.00000 cm now).
- Each bone must be set from its pre-turn read, never turned twice.
- A shut gate (`Fix.Num` 0) must still set nothing.
- Do not "fix" it by reading the shoulders from `Raw`: that pose lacks the hips' drop, the lean and the look that `StepPosture` must measure.
- Stage 4 must still read the forearm and hand after the upper arm is set, so they follow.

Correct the comment at line 320 and the header promise at .h 167-170 to say what is actually set.

**To guard it:** `tests/posture.cpp`'s `EngineTurn` assumes everything follows, so the harness needs a small read/set/convert pose model, with the stage's read and set lists as data, run over the five gaits. The sabotage is dropping the clavicles from the set list; it must fail at 4.14 cm on Walk_Brisk.
