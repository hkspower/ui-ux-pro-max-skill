### Their reasoning
I tried to refute it and could not.

- **Unity really lacks it.** `GuardTarget` (FighterIK.cs:458-462) adds the constants `GuardHeight` 1.50, `GuardForward` 0.26 and `GuardSpread` 0.11 to the root with no size term. `FindFoot` (line 327) reads `transform.localScale.y`, the root's scale. `SetStature` (EnemyFighter.cs:101) scales only the `Mesh` child, and Bootstrap.cs:170-173 says the root is deliberately never scaled.
- **The inputs are the game's own.** DistrictRuntime and WaveDirector call `Instantiate`, then `ConfigureFrom` -> `SetStature(def.scale)` before FighterIK's `Start`. fighters.json has scales from 0.94 to 1.55, and the big men guard: Bouncer `guardChance` 0.34, Boss 0.52, Zayos 0.42. `Models/Ahmed.fbx` exists, so `MakeBody` does attach FighterIK to enemies.
- **A player would see it.** The guard comment promises the hands "up at the chin" and calls the guard "the one piece of state the player most needs to read off a body". On ZAYOS the block puts both fists in front of his belt, 82.5 cm under his chin and 7.9 cm below where they hang at rest. The Boss and Bouncer carry them at the belly, 36-39 cm under the chin.
- **Unreal really has it.** Its CLAUDE.md section "The IK at their size" (line 4768) records every length band growing with the man. SaudMotionAnimInstance.cpp passes `BodyScale` to the feet, `Cover`, `SkinOf`, `MarkPoint` and `BlockStandOff`. Its guard is the Block clip on the scaled skeleton, so it sits at the man's own chin by construction.
- **Not recorded as decided.** `SetStature`'s comment records only that reach does not grow. Commit 8ef098c added the scaling after the guard existed and never mentions the guard. Unity's CLAUDE.md says of the hold "distances grow with the root's scale"; that describes the broken wiring, not a decision to leave big men unscaled, since `HoldFoot`'s own doc and Hold.cs both promise "with the man". The port is frozen, but the task says its owner allows IK changes.
- **It fits FighterIK.cs and the Mono harness.** The trial above changes only FighterIK.cs and every existing suite still passes.

One part I only read rather than ran in an engine: that a scaled `Mesh` child puts the bones at root + scale x joint. That is standard Unity transform behaviour, but the harness Transform does not compose scale, so both my probe and the finder's place the bones there by hand. The finder's probe is sound on this point and its numbers match mine to the millimetre.

The hold half of the finding is the weaker one visually: ZAYOS shuffles at 12 cm instead of 18.6. It is a broken promise more than a striking fault; the guard is what a player would notice.

### Their fix advice
Smallest sound fix, all inside FighterIK.cs, as trialled in `fixtrial/FighterIK.cs`:

1. **One size per fighter, measured from the skeleton each LateUpdate.** Add `private float _size = 1f` and set it right after `_mesh.localPosition = _meshRest`: the mean of `transform.InverseTransformPoint(leg.Root.position).y` over the bound legs, divided by `HipHeight` (0.952); use 1 when no leg is bound or the result is not positive. This mirrors Unreal's Size = leg / SaudLegLength. I prefer it to the finder's `_mesh.localScale.y` for three reasons: the harness Transform defaults `localScale` to zero and does not compose it; nobody has verified the imported FBX root's scale is 1; and measuring per frame also covers a `SetStature` that runs after `Start`.
2. **Guard.** `GuardTarget(origin, facing, side, float size = 1f)` returns `origin + (facing*GuardForward + side*GuardSpread + up*GuardHeight) * s`, with non-positive size taken as 1. `GuardHand` passes `_size`.
3. **Feet.** `FindFoot` passes `_size` to `HoldFoot` in place of `Mathf.Abs(transform.localScale.y)`, and targets `AnkleHeight * _size + lift`, so flat ground is a no-op for a scaled man as it is for Ahmed.

What it must not break:
- **Size 1 stays exactly as today.** tests/IK.cs:120-122 calls the three-argument `GuardTarget` and compares it to `GuardHeight`; tests/Pose.cs:180 checks the hand at `GuardHeight`. Keep the default parameter. The hip-height measure gives exactly 1 on the Pose rig.
- **Strike reach does not grow.** `StrikeTarget` keeps `row.reach` and the `ShoulderHeight`/`HipHeight` landing heights; `SetStature`'s comment records that as decided.
- **`HoldFoot` itself is untouched.** Its signature and the size-1.5 and size-0 cases in tests/Hold.cs stay; only the caller's argument changes.
- **Do not scale the root.** Bootstrap records why: the controller takes the transform's scale with it.

Scaling `WindUpDraw`, `FlinchOffset`'s caps, `RayStart` and `MaxHipDrop` by the same size is optional and not needed for the visible fault.

Unity's CLAUDE.md line "distances grow with the root's scale" should be changed to say the man's size.

Test: rigs at 0.94, 1.26 and 1.55 with bones at scaled places. Blocking, each wrist is within 5 cm of 1.50 x size up and lifted more than 15 cm; a 1.55 man carried 15 cm keeps his foot while a 1.00 man steps. Sabotages: size fixed at 1; size read from the root again; height scaled but not forward and spread.
