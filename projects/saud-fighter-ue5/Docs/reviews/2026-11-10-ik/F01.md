### Their reasoning
Real, with two corrections to the finding.

The cause is confirmed line by line. SaudMotionAnimInstance.cpp:335 reads the clip's knee from CS after the pelvis (and posture) moved it, and :341 passes it as the pole. SaudIK.h:194 takes the bend as that knee's offset from the NEW hip-to-target line. Saud's mocap walk ends its swing with the knee 1.07 cm off its line; when the feet's pass lowers the hips 3.5 cm and lifts the foot 3 cm, the line swings past the knee and the bend direction is whatever is left.

Realistic inputs: the clip, plants, speed and gait are the game's own (0.45 x 341 = 153 cm/s, which PickGait gives to A_Saud_Mocap_Walk between 122 and 162). The ground is L_MonkeyIsland's landscape: build_monkey_island.py sets TRAIL_GRADE = 0.18 (10.2 deg) and the hills run steeper. The fault starts at about 9 deg, so it shows on the trail's steepest stretches and on the hills, not on gentle ground.

Correction 1: the finder's probe omits the posture stage, which is on by default in Idle and Walk (bPostureStraight = true). Without it I reproduce the backward flip to the decimal. With it, at the trail's grade the knee is not behind its line; it bows 14-16 cm sideways once a stride, about 82 deg off its own bend, with a small jump. It goes backwards as well from about 15 deg. Either way it is a knee bending the wrong way that a player would see, from the same line of code. Posture was modelled from TurnBones as read, not compiled.

Correction 2: the kerb half is not shown on game data. The city's 6 cm paving is laid solid=False (build_world.py:422) and :1534-1535 turns its collision off, so neither the capsule nor the IK's traces see it. The 10 and 15 cm kerbs are harness fixtures; I found no kerb with collision in the world data. The kerb numbers reproduce as arithmetic only.

CLAUDE.md: the four IK sections, the 360 section, the posture section and "Known, not fixed" record nothing about the leg's pole or a knee's side on a slope. The posture section's "steps and slopes made no fault" is about sideways lean, not knees.

Not covered: the other men's built walks (no joint data in the harness) and sizes other than Saud's (the mocap gaits are his alone). Probes: vprobe.cpp (finder's model, kerbs, guards), vprobe2.cpp (posture stage, frame dump), vprobe3.cpp (the fix), all in the scratch directory.

### Their fix advice
Smallest sound fix: leave SaudIK::TwoBone alone and change only the pole passed at SaudMotionAnimInstance.cpp:341.

Add an engine-free helper to SaudIK.h, e.g. LegPole(Hip, Knee, Ankle, Target, FootFwd, LegLength):
- Take the leg's OWN bend: the knee's offset from its own Hip-to-Ankle line, using Hip, Knee and the foot's location as read from CS just before the solve. That is after the pelvis and the posture, so the pelvis roll is carried with the leg; this matters, because measuring against the raw clip or the new line leaves the sideways bow.
- Where that offset is under about 0.5 cm (a ruler-straight leg), use the sole's forward made square to the line. Blend between the two over roughly 0.5-2 cm rather than switching, so the direction cannot jump.
- Swing it with SaudIK::Swing from (Ankle - Hip) onto (Target - Hip).
- Return Hip + Bend * LegLength, and pass that in place of Knee.

Measured on this fix: 0 frames behind and at most 3.9 deg off its own bend from 0 to 20 deg at 30/60/120 Hz, with and without the posture stage.

It must not break:
- A leg the feet do not move still solves to the clip's knee exactly (0.0 deg off on the flat), so the feet's on/off ramp and "alpha 0 is the clip" stay free of pops.
- TwoBone's existing checks (pole's side, pole on the line, 72 angles, MaxStretch) stay untouched.
- The clips' designed knee directions, including the bosses' turned-out rear knees, are kept; never substitute a fixed forward pole.
- The strike's leg still takes over from the feet's solve with no jump (section 4 reads CS after).
- The arms' SolveLimb calls, which use the elbow as the pole, are a separate matter; leave them.

The test should run the mocap walk up 10.2 and 15 deg at 30/60/120 Hz both without and WITH the posture's pelvis turn applied to the leg. It should hold the knee never more than 1 cm behind its line and within about 30 deg of its own bend. Sabotage with today's call: 9 backward times and a 31.5 cm jump without posture, 14.9 cm sideways with it. Not compilable against the engine here; say so.
