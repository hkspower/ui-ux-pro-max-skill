### Their reasoning
I tried to break it and could not; both halves reproduce, the knee half strongly.

Line by line: FighterIK.Strike() (lines 596-610) sends limb.End of every row to StrikeTarget(root, facing, row.reach, HipHeight or ShoulderHeight) and reads nothing about any opponent. The Knee row's reach (1.008 m) puts that target 101 cm from the hip of an 88 cm leg, so TwoBoneIK straightens it: the knee strike is drawn as the kick, 2.2 degrees apart. Facing is fixed for the whole swing (FightStyleRunner.Update returns while IsBusy; PlayerFighter.ReadInput likewise), and Fighter.InHitbox accepts a man up to 0.8 m off the line.

The inputs are ones the callers produce. PlayerFighter.Kick throws Knee whenever a man is inside 1.0 m, and two 0.32 m controllers touch at 0.64 m, so 0.66-0.99 m is the whole range of Ahmed's knee. At every distance in it the ankle is drawn past the man's front, and through his middle up to about 0.85 m, for 100-300 ms a swing, 60-90 ms of it frozen by hit stop. Champ, Boss and Saqr throw it in their styles' Close band (up to 1.0-1.12 m) with the same result.

Unreal has the behaviour: StrikeOf gives Knee the knee as its tip, the anim instance (SaudMotionAnimInstance.cpp 395-430) calls KneeAim/KneeSwing and ReachableMark/MeetPoint/SwingGate/SolveStrike, and my probe on the header shows the arm solved 0.0 degrees off a man 20-28 degrees aside and the knee leading with the ankle well short of his front.

Not recorded as decided or left. The Unity CLAUDE.md describes the mechanism ("drives the striking limb to the attack row's reach") but nowhere says a knee drawn as a straight leg, or an unaimed blow, is accepted. The Unreal CLAUDE.md lists the same defect ("the knee strike pulled the FOOT to the man") under "What was wrong before", fixed 2026-10-02. tests/IK.cs pins only the pure StrikeTarget ("lands at its reach").

It fits FighterIK.cs and the harness: GatherTargets and InHitbox are public, and my probe ran two rigs through Scene.Step with the real hit resolution.

Corrections to the finder's numbers; none changes the verdict:
- Contact-frame depths are about 2.8 cm overstated. Hit stop freezes the attacker before LateUpdate applies the lunge, so at 0.90 m the ankle is 9.8 cm past his front and 2.2 cm before his middle, not 12.6 and 0.6 past.
- The Grappler and Bouncer "ring" distances (0.883 m, 1.00 m) are EnemyFighter.TickAI's, which styled fighters never run. Their styles throw Knee only in Close (0.577 m and 0.652 m or less), inside the controllers' touch distances (0.698 m, 0.717 m), so those two cases are not ones the game produces. Champ, Boss and Saqr are.
- The Runner "0.70 m aside, 34.1 degrees" case exceeds his circling (0.8 x 5.04 m/s gives 0.54 m, 25.8 degrees). A walking victim gives 18-26 degrees at full strafe and about 10 at half. Roughly 33 degrees does occur in a crowd: ResolveHits lands a non-multiHit blow on the first listed man in the box, not the one faced.
- Aiming changes direction only. The arm is 0.60 m and the men stand 1.1-1.2 m apart, so even dead ahead the wrist stops about 38 cm short of his front. The aim half is visible (the wrist moves 35-68 cm across) but less glaring than the knee half.

### Their fix advice
Keep it inside FighterIK.Strike() and new pure statics beside StrikeTarget, in two steps. Do the knee first; it is the certain, high-visibility half.

1. The knee. Let StrikeLimb also say which point lands (end or knee), still as lead/rear. For Knee, aim the knee joint: K = hip + dir x thigh, where dir runs from the hip to a point on the man's front (his middle minus 0.12 x his size toward the striker, about plexus height), stopped 5 cm x the striker's size short. When that point is nearer than a thigh, raise K up his front instead. With nobody in the box, use the facing line, raised. Blend from the rest knee by StrikeWeight, on the arc. Then call TwoBoneIK.Apply(Root, Mid, End, K + shin x (down and back), pole = K). My prototype on the real Solve lands the knee joint 0.0 cm from K with both lengths kept, for sizes 0.94-1.55.

2. The aim. Find the man from state the fight already keeps: _fighter.GatherTargets into one reused list, only while State == Attack (PlayerFighter.GatherTargets allocates). Use the same filters and the same order as Fighter.ResolveHits (alive, not Down, InHitbox with the row's reach and depthTolerance, first match), so the limb is drawn to the man the blow actually lands on, not merely the nearest. Take his size from his CharacterController height / 1.8. Swing the limb's line from the facing line toward the point on his front at the row's landing height, gated by the angle off the facing line (full to 30, none at 55 for an arm; 60 and 90 for a leg) and multiplied by StrikeWeight.

Must not break:
- Hit resolution (Fighter.ResolveHits / InHitbox) is not touched; this is pose only.
- With nobody in the box the wrist or ankle is where it is today, to the millimetre. StrikeTarget, StrikeWeight, WindUp and the lead/rear map keep their signatures and the tests/IK.cs checks ("lands at its reach", the timeline, the stance map).
- The striking leg stays out of HoldFoot and HipDrop (tests/Hold.cs, Pose.cs). IKDebug's StrikeOn / StrikeTarget / StrikeWeight show the aimed target.
- The port's rules: no new field on Fighter, TwoBoneIK.Solve stays pure, no left/right in the strike map.
- Segment lengths are kept. Nothing of the leg passes his front plane on a Knee. A man past the gate is not struck round the body.

For the test:
- The harness's Object.FindObjectsByType returns an empty array and a PlayerFighter cannot be built without GameData. Use a test subclass of Fighter with its own GatherTargets in a two-rig Pose-style scene; my probe does exactly this and the real ResolveHits runs through it.
- Do not assert that a fist touches the man: the arm is 0.60 m and the men stand 1.1-1.2 m apart. Assert direction (shoulder-to-wrist within a few degrees of the line to him inside the gate) and, for the knee, that the knee joint is under about 120 degrees, leads the ankle, and nothing passes his front.
- Use knee distances the game produces: 0.66-0.99 m for Ahmed; Champ, Boss or Saqr for an enemy. Not the Grappler's or Bouncer's TickAI ring.
