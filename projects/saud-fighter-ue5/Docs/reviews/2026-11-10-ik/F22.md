The guard ignores a second man's blow until the first man's whole swing is over

finder's probe: <scratch>/ik/find-unreal-arms/block2.cpp

## Scenario
Two men swing at a guarding fighter (SaudGameplay::MaxSimultaneousAttackers is 2). The first man's blow starts at 0; the second man's jab is timed to go live 0.10 s after the first blow's active frames end, that is 20 ms after nothing of the first blow shows (its schedule runs out 0.08 s after its active frames). The probe drives the real SaudIK::FBlockTrack and ContactAlpha with UpdateBlock's choosing transcribed, at 60 Hz. FBlockTrack and the comment at the call promise 'the next taken as soon as nothing of the last shows'.

## Evidence (finder)
Code: 'if (Held && Held->State == EFighterState::Attack && ... ) { Att = Incoming(Held); From = Att ? Held : nullptr; }' then 'if (!From && BlockTrack.Free())' -- while the first man is still in his recovery From stays set, so nobody else is looked for though Free() is true. Printed: after a Jab the guard's share for the second jab is 0.00 at its first active frame and 0.58 at most over its active frames; after a Cross, a Hook or a Kick it is 0.00 at its first active frame and 0.00 over all its active frames. The guard takes the second jab only when the first man's swing ends: 0.167 / 0.233 / 0.253 s into a jab that is live from 0.070 to 0.130 s, so after it has landed, and then only twitches as it lets go. With the sketch (look again once the held blow shows nothing) the share reaches 1.00 inside the jab's active frames in all four cases (0.19-0.58 at its first active frame).

## Finder's fix sketch
In UpdateBlock drop the held blow once it is past its first active frame and BlockTrack.Free() is true (its schedule has run out), then take the incoming blow furthest into its swing from anyone but that man; keep the rest as it is, so a blow still showing is never cut.
