### Their reasoning
Real, and it is a hole inside the Unreal build, not a Unity parity item. The finder's kind "gap" is mislabelled: I judged it as "the code promises X and does not do it". Unity's FighterIK.cs has no posture stage at all, and nothing in this finding belongs in Unity.

Confirmed line by line (the engine side cannot be compiled, only read):
- WaveDirector.cpp:351 sets `Enemy->MotionSet = Row;` unconditionally; it is the only writer of MotionSet in Source, and DT_Fighters has no motion-set column.
- SaudMotionAnimInstance.cpp:80-83 `SetOf` returns that row name; line 1278 passes it to `SaudStances::Find`, an exact match over five names (Saud, Street, Boss, Saqr, Zayos).
- A null stance turns the stage off twice: line 1289 (`StanceOf != nullptr`) and the proxy's line 284 (`if (Frame.Stance && bLegs)`).
- The street rows do stand in Street's guard: SaudMotionComponent.cpp:208-219 borrows A_Street_* for any set without its own clip, and no A_Thug_ (etc.) clip exists on disk.

The promise is explicit:
- CLAUDE.md "Posture and alignment -- 2026-10-07": "settled as: everyone".
- SaudIK.h:2246: "everyone -- Saud, the bosses, the street men".
- The call site's own comments (cpp:1273, .h:101) name only the Island's creatures as having no stance.

The sibling lookups on the same key do handle a row name: `SetSwingSeconds` falls back to Street (SaudIK.h:2230-2232), ik.cpp:2009-2011 checks "a street row's are Street's", and `StrikeOf(Row, Set)` treats any non-creature set as a man. tests/posture.cpp only ever asks for the five set names, so nothing catches this.

Not recorded as decided or known: none of the four IK sections, the posture section or "Known, not fixed" mentions it. CLAUDE.md line 1299 itself says the director sets MotionSet to the fighter's row.

A player sees it. Eight of the eleven enemy rows, the bulk of the game's enemies, keep the pre-2026-10-07 faults that Saud and the three bosses had taken out: hips 16-23 degrees round on held feet when turned on the spot, and the head tipped 19-20 degrees with the lean when chasing across.

I read the finder's probe critically. Its inputs are the tests' own fixtures and the real CSV speeds, and its numbers match mine (16.6 -> 2.5 at 90 deg/s, 60 Hz).

Caveats:
- My chase is an idealised pure pursuit with no acceleration model, so the lean reaches its 20-degree limit near the end.
- Like tests/posture.cpp, the turning case does not model the hips' yaw lag.
- Nothing here was compiled against the engine.

### Their fix advice
Unreal only; no Unity change belongs to this finding.

Smallest sound fix: resolve the stance by the same borrowing rule the clips and the shuffle already use, in engine-free code so the harness executes it.
- Add e.g. `inline const SaudStances::FStance* StanceFor(const char* Set)` to SaudIK.h: `Find(Set)`; if that is null and Set is not "Monkey" or "Gorilla", `Find("Street")`. This mirrors `SetSwingSeconds` (SaudIK.h:2227-2232) and `USaudMotionComponent::Find`.
- Call it at SaudMotionAnimInstance.cpp:1278 in place of `SaudStances::Find`.
- Do not hand-edit SaudStances.h. It is generated by Tools/blender/measure_stances.py, which writes `Find` itself and has a `--check`. Put the resolver in SaudIK.h, or change the generator and regenerate.

It must not break:
- Monkey and Gorilla still resolve to null (another skeleton, "left as their clips have them").
- Saud, Boss, Saqr and Zayos still get their own stance, not Street's (AL-WAHSH keeps his 4.9-degree shoulder).
- The once-per-set cache (StanceSet / bStanceLooked) and `SetOf`'s "Saud" for a None set stay as they are.
- A future set that has its own guard clip but no stance row should still be caught by `measure_stances.py --check`, not silently take Street's.
- tests/posture.cpp's existing checks and its 36 sabotages still pass.

Test, in tests/posture.cpp:
- Read DT_Fighters.csv and DT_IslandFighters.csv. Every men's row resolves through the new function; the eight street rows resolve to "Street"; the island rows resolve to null.
- Run the curve case for "Thug" through the resolver and check his eyes end under 1.5 degrees.
- Sabotage: drop the fallback; eight rows go null and the Thug's eyes stay about 16-20 degrees.

Remaining edge, acceptable: a project whose Street clips were never imported plays A_Saud_* as the last resort and would be measured against Street's stance.
