## Scenario
FinishFoot rolls a held foot's heel up whenever the ankle is further from the hip than LegLength x HoldReach (98.5 %), meant for a body carried away from a planted foot. It never asks whether the CLIP itself has the leg straighter than that. The Street boxer's, AL-SAQR's and ZAYOS's guards stand on a lead leg at 99.90, 99.93 and 99.21 % of its length (SaudStances.h, frame 0 of each guard). Probe: each of the five real guards at its size, standing still on flat ground for 2 s with both feet held, nothing else happening, 30/60/120 Hz.

## Evidence (finder)
probe_roll (the same at 30, 60 and 120 Hz): Street x0.95 lead knee 174.9 deg in the clip, drawn 160.1 deg, the knee moved 5.7 cm, heel rolled 4.3 deg and 1.27 cm off the floor; AL-SAQR x1.10 lead 175.7 -> 160.1 deg, knee moved 7.0 cm, heel 5.2 deg and 1.52 cm up; ZAYOS x1.48 lead 165.6 -> 160.1 deg, knee 3.3 cm, heel 1.00 cm up. Saud's and the Boss's guards (93-95 %) and every rear leg are drawn as the clip has them (0.0 cm, 0.0 deg). The clips' plants mark those lead feet flat ('f') in every frame.

## Finder's fix sketch
Measure the reach against what the clip itself asks: Reach = LegLength x max(HoldReach, min(clip's own hip-to-ankle share, MaxStretch)), the way TwoBone's Longest already honours ClipReach; or roll only by what the held ball's offset from the clip's place adds. A guard's designed straight lead leg then keeps its heel down and only TwoBone's 99.5 % limit touches the knee.

## Finder's test sketch

The existing 'the roll brings the ankle back inside 98.5 %' check still holds for a man carried off his foot. Sabotage: Reach = LegLength x HoldReach whatever the clip (today's line) -- the Street guard rolls 4.3 degrees and lifts its heel 1.27 cm.
