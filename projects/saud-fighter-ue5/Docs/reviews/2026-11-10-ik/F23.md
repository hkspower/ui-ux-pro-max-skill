## Scenario
A fighter not rendered for a while (behind the swung camera) who has meanwhile walked onto ground of another height -- a 15 cm kerb here -- and then comes into view. The file's own comments give the premise: while unrendered 'the pose is not refreshed ... though the update runs', so NativeUpdateAnimation keeps tracing but FSaudFeetBack stays as the last Evaluate left it, and on the first Evaluate back the proxy takes `bMissed` (gap > 0.5 s), resets the feet and clamps Dt to 0.25. The same line serves the frame of a door teleport (bTeleported is known on the game thread but the traces still go to the old place). Probe: ik.cpp's Man fixture with the engine's trace window (floor +60 / -70 cm) and the capsule's 2.15 cm float, standing 1 s at x = -150 on the road, unseen while he goes to x = +150 on the kerb's other side, then the first frame as the engine runs it (traces at Back's tracks, In.bTeleported, Dt 0.25) against the same frame traced under him; sizes 0.95 / 1.00 / 1.48 at 30 / 60 / 120 Hz.

## Finder's fix sketch
Trace under the mesh's own sockets whenever Back is not last update's: stamp FSaudFeetBack with the Frame.Clock it was evaluated at, keep the previous update's clock on the instance, and use the GetSocketLocation branch when `!Back.bValid || bTeleported || Back.Clock != PreviousClock` (the sockets ride the component's current transform, so they are under him even with a stale pose). That one test covers the door, the respawn and the unseen man.
